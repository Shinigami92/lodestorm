# Sound

Weighty and real, no cartoon or chiptune: human gear clanks, hisses and hums (industrial, worn), weapons punchy but not arcade. The menus are silent for now (their first sounds were dropped after listening; they come back with their own round). All levels **TBD (playtest)**.

- **Made by our own scripts:** every sound is synthesized by Python scripts in the repo (oscillators, noise, envelopes, filters, layered), deterministic, so it can be rebuilt or tuned any time and is entirely the project's own. Recordings (CC0, or CC-BY with a credit; never NC, ND or SA) come in only where synthesis isn't convincing, processed by the same scripts; none are used yet. **All of them are placeholders**, a first proof of the sound: a better one replaces a sound's files under the same names, and nothing in the game changes. New sounds come in 2–3 **takes**, picked by ear on a local listening page; only the picked one stays.
- **Variation:** sounds that repeat have 2–6 variants and a small random pitch (±3–5 %), so shots never sound like a loop.
- **Loops** (the drill, the hose, the repair, the storm, a furnace's roar) play seamlessly as long as what makes them goes on, from where it happens (the storm all around).
- **Where the ear is:** the game is heard from the local player (not from the camera far above), turned like the camera, so left and right match the screen and the falloff fits what's on screen. Sounds in the world come from where they happen (the gun, the player) and stop while the game is paused; flat sounds (a volume slider's sample) go on. In co-op each player hears from their own player; VR (M6) puts the ears at the head.
- **When it gets busy:** each sound has a limit of how many play at once (shots 4–6, cues 1); when more start, the farthest are dropped. **Critical cues** for the local player always play and duck the effects and ambience by 6 dB while they do: the timing window's tick, a hit taken, the low-health pulse, the storm warning. A teammate's timing cues and pulse aren't heard.
- **Playable muted:** nothing is told only by sound; everything a sound says is also shown in the world (the suit ring, the light, the cards).
- **Volumes:** buses Master → Effects, Ambience, Voice (the NPCs' voices) → Radio (crew calls, through a band-pass filter and a little distortion, so the same voice sounds like a radio; it follows "Voices" until it gets its own slider with the comms log), later Interface with the menus' sounds and Music; a slider each (10 % steps) on the settings' Sound page ("Volume", "Effects", "Ambience", "Voices"), saved with the other settings; moving "Volume" or "Effects" plays a shot as a sample, "Ambience" the storm, "Voices" Brannik asking "How can I help you?" in the voice language (so does picking a voice language).
- **The sounds so far:**
  - **Weapons:** Harl's, Brask's, Pinner's, Grindle's and Quill's shots, Dray's swing and its hit (only when it strikes something), Riven's slash, its heavy third slash and its hit, Auger's thrust, its hit and its drill (a loop rising with it), Jag's saw (a loop following its blade) and its bite into what it touches, Clout's punch (the pistons' bang), its heavy hit, its charge (a straining servo whine rising with it) and the latch when it's full, a thrown alien landing and slamming into rock, Mantle's bash and its hit, its field's quiet hum (a loop while it stands), a blow or shot stopped (deeper for a heavy one), the field breaking and coming back, Grindle's barrels spinning (a loop, its pitch following the spin), Quill's charge (a hum rising with it) and a ping when it's full, Tremor's hollow launch thunk, a blast (Tremor's shells, Wick's charges), Wick's toss, a charge armed (two low beeps) and tripped (fast rising beeps), Scald's roaring flame, Kith's soft, warm beam and Zenith's searing drone (loops while they fire), an overheat's ping and alarm, a vent's hiss, a fuel cell seated, Fulgen's crack of lightning, Nadir's deep whomp, a gravity well's throbbing hum (a loop while it's open) and its burst, Pinner's battery full again after running dry, Nadir's soft chime for each well recharged, the empty click (a reload that can't start, a drained battery), the reload starting, a magazine seated, each shell going in; for the local player the window's tick and whether the timing press hit or missed.
  - **Player:** a hit taken (a thud, the armor plate clanking, sparks), a slow pulse below a quarter of the health (a heartbeat every 1.1 s), the dash (a rising whoosh), and subtle **steps**: one every 2 m walked on the ground (in time with the run clips' legs; none while dashing or in the air), a boot on gravel outside, on the metal deck aboard (the ship's floors and its ramp), crunching on glass shards (the Glass plains), squelching in mud (the Overgrowth). Teammates' steps carry only a few meters. Death has no sound yet (the synthesized takes didn't work).
  - **Aliens** (the hive wet, clicking and chittering; every strain in its own voice, bigger ones lower, all with the swarmer's sounds for now): a chitter when one notices a player and every 3–7 s while it chases (not under the ground or while hidden), its bite or slash landing, hits in its flesh (a wet thwack) or on its armor (a hollow knock), its death. Every heavy attack's **telegraph** sounds with its wind-up, rising into the strike: the pounce (a hissing rattle), the charge (a snort and a growl), the slam (a deep groan), the spit (acid gurgling), the bloater swelling, the shrieker drawing breath and then its **scream**, the stalker's hook creaking, the darter's wings buzzing, the burrower rumbling under the ground, the Ravager's hide tearing open.
  - **Nests:** a deep, wet throb with each pulse of the glow, faster and louder as the hive escalates; a wet pop for each alien hatching (not the guards it starts with); hits in its flesh; a groaning swell for a burst's warning; the collapse (a crunch, the mound caving in, gas hissing out, its hum dying).
  - **Mining:** a miner set down (a thud, the clamps, a hiss), its servos while it's placed or picked up (a whining motor and whirring gears, rising in pitch as it turns real, falling as it fades; quiet while it waits for its player), the latches snapping shut when it's picked up, its drill while it mines (a motor humming, the bit grinding), the hose while ore flows (a dull rush, a pump, grains rattling), a soft two-tone chime when it's full, an alarm when it breaks down (its motor stalling with a bang, a warning buzz), the repair while a player mends it (a ratchet, a wrench on steel, a torch).
  - **Mission:** the ship's ramp moving (latches, hydraulics, the clunk at the end); for the local player the storm's warning (a deep two-tone horn, a critical cue), the suit's countdown beeps (every 10 s of the last minute, then every second of the last 10), the storm hitting when the time is up (a crack, a blast, a roar), and the storm itself all around on the Ambience bus, as loud as its haze and muffled aboard (wind gusting with grit, a deep roar).
  - **Refinery:** a furnace igniting when a batch starts (the igniter ticking, the gas catching with a whump, the burners swelling), its low roar while it runs (a loop on the Ambience bus, heard close by), the burners choking off with a valve clunking shut when one is stopped, and a bar clinking onto the rack when one is done.
  - **Ore exchange:** a trade (a hopper's flap clunking open, ore chunks rattling down the funnel, a dull thud below).
  - **Biomes:** the Ash fields' vents rumble rising for their warning and burst with a roar of gas; the shards and the mud have their own steps.
