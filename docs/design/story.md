# Story and setting (draft)

- **Setup:** a colony in a newly opened star system runs short of supplies. The players are a prospecting crew supplying it, next to other crews, stations and outposts.
- **The mystery:** a first expedition went missing. Following its trail reveals bit by bit what the players don't know at the start: the storms come from the system's star, which is becoming unstable, and they keep growing stronger. The rare ores only form where its radiation is strongest.
- **The ending:** the colony has to leave the system before a big flare, which needs something built from the rarest ores near the star: a new lane beacon with a flare shield around it, so the colony's haulers can cross the lane at the system's edge before the flare (the players' heat shield is the flare shield's small prototype). So the campaign moves inward, planet by planet. The six acts, the expedition's fate, the people and the post-game: see [`planned/story.md`](../planned/story.md).
- **How it's told:** NPCs and their quests drive the main story, in conversations aboard that the players start themselves. During runs, NPCs speak over the radio only when it matters out there (the storm warning, quest updates, story moments), each call from the one it concerns, so the radio never becomes noise; everything else they say aboard. Players in co-op may talk over it (e.g. on voice chat), so nothing important is only said over the radio: radio lines stay short and can be listened to again aboard (a comms log). The expedition's logs, found as handcrafted pieces on the planets, are optional lore for players who want to dig deeper; the main story never depends on reading them. Cutscenes are unlikely (costly); key moments can use the camera transitions that exist anyway (e.g. the ship taking off and landing).
- **Between missions** (the story director and the messages, built in 16 A3; what comes next: Between missions in [`planned/story.md`](../planned/story.md)): the world reacts between runs through **beats**, each something new in the story (a topic, a quest, a message) that waits for its conditions: story flags, a quest's step, the act (one per planet, ending with its heat shield plate), runs since the last beat or since this one, the heat shield's next plate paid (a third, two thirds), an ore found, a region visited, reputation with an NPC, players falling in the run just ended; never the wall clock. On every **return** (the end of a run, won or lost, or docking at a station) the director puts out the best beat that's ready (by priority; some only on a run's return or a docking, some only at one body or station). A beat waits until what it brings is done (e.g. its topic heard), so at most two wait at once and only one per NPC; a repeating one (a request coming again) never waits. Quests stay their own system; the beats only open them. **Messages** come in on the same returns once their flags are set (usually a beat's): short voiced messages left for the ship while it was away, listed on the quest board's Messages tab and played aloud there, heard once for the whole ship (they stay to be heard again). Someone at a station who waits for the players (a beat they carry, or a quest step handed in at them with all its goals done) gets an amber diamond on their station in the navigator and **calls** the ship once: a message of their own on the board ("Selin here. There's something I'd like to discuss with you. Come and see me when you dock."; three lines each, never the one before), not when the ship is docked there, and not again while their last call is unheard. **Overheard talks** (built in 16 B5): two people talking to each other at a place aboard or at a station, for whoever comes close. Once its flags are set (usually a beat's), both walk to the talk's spot (Fenna and Tamsin at the mess table, Dace and Okoro at the weapons bench) and wait there, side by side facing each other and turned a little to the front, without a marker: seeing them together is the hint. A player coming within about 6 m starts it: each line in the speaker's bubble and voice, one bubble at a time, as long as a line moving on by itself; it plays to the end whether anyone stays or walks on, and once only. While it plays neither can be talked to, both keep facing each other, and their name plates and markers hide; a talk to one of them while they wait holds it until that talk is over. An important one is kept, to be heard again on the quest board's Logs tab (16 D); the others aren't kept anywhere, so whatever the story needs also comes as a topic or a message. There are no beats, messages or talks in the game yet (the first come with the quests' content); Selin, Kade, Nell and Rook have their calls (Rook unvoiced).
- **One star system** for the campaign: six planets from the outside in, one per signature ore (the fourth a gas giant whose two moons carry its ore), four moons, stations and outposts; built so more systems can follow later (see [Missions](missions.md), Planets, moons and regions).
- **Length:** about 40 h to beat the game; play can go on afterwards (post-game, e.g. higher difficulties with rarer ores, open).
