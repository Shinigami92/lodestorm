# Weapons (planned: the rest)

Decided 2026-10-17. All values **TBD (playtest)**. The 18 weapons, their feeds, parts, the draw, blueprints, building them at the workbench and the racks are built (M4 step 10, see [Weapons](../design/weapons.md#weapons) and [Upgrade stations](../design/progression.md#upgrade-stations)); what's left comes with the quests, the art pass, VR and co-op.

## Getting them

- **Blueprints from quests** (see [`quests.md`](quests.md)): colony arms and tools from the crew's quest chains, the six prototypes from the lost expedition's sites (story). In co-op everyone in the session gets the blueprint and builds it from their own hold. Until then the debug overlay gives them.
- **Order over the campaign** (about 120 runs over 40 h, so a new one every 6–7 runs): start: Harl, Dray. Torvane (and Lisk): Brask (the quartermaster's chain), Riven, Pinner. Vehra: Grindle, Wick, Clout. Cassor (and Mirr): Quill, Kith, Auger. Gaulm's moons: Tremor, Fulgen, Mantle. Skelde: Scald, Jag, Nadir. Zairen: Zenith. Early weapons are plain, later ones exotic and paid with rarer ores.

## Still to come

- **A third loadout slot** later, as a skill deep in a tree (rare ores); slots stay 2 until then, since choosing from many weapons is the point.
- **Projectiles tell them apart** (art pass): each weapon its own shot shape, trail and impact, besides its silhouette.
- **Co-op:** Kith's beam revives downed teammates from 5 m (see [`coop-revive.md`](coop-revive.md)); Mantle also shields the sentries (see [`skills.md`](skills.md)).
- **VR:** every weapon aims with one direction (mouse or stick on desktop, the hand in VR); none needs precise aim beyond the screen. Melee swings on the button in VR too, for now.
