# Statistics (planned)

Decided 2026-10-23. The events, the counters, the totals and the session logs are built (Statistics A, see [`TECHNICAL.md`](../TECHNICAL.md#statistics)). What's left: a live view for development and the statistics for players. All values **TBD (playtest)**.

- **Live `/metrics` endpoint (B):** for watching the numbers live while playing (Prometheus scraping, Grafana showing them). Off by default and only for development: switched on by a command-line flag and/or a developer setting (which of them, and the port, decided when it's built), serving only on `127.0.0.1`. The game answers `GET /metrics` in the Prometheus text format from a `TCPServer` polled every frame (no thread, never blocking the game). The recorder's counters become `lodestorm_<counter>_total` with their labels (each counter gets the names of its labels for this, e.g. `weapon`, `strain`), the seconds and meters too; live gauges on top (health, aliens alive, nests left, the mission's time left, frames per second).
- **Statistics station (C):** the player's statistics over all sessions on a 3D panel aboard (a station like the others, see [UI](../GAME_DESIGN.md#ui-in-the-world-not-on-the-screen)): kills per weapon and strain, accuracy of the timing presses, deaths and their causes, ore, runs; it stands in the specimen lab in the lower nose (see [`ship-interior.md`](ship-interior.md)); what it shows is a design round first. Only for what the player has found (no locked weapon or unseen strain named, like the silos' "?").
- **Achievements (M8):** counted from the same counters; Steam Stats mirror them (see [`TECHNICAL.md`](../TECHNICAL.md#achievements)).
