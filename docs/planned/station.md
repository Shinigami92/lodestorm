# Waypost Ostra (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 8 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. Today Waypost Ostra is in the star system's data and on the navigator as a station shape ("No docking yet"); stations and outposts in general: see [Spaceship](../GAME_DESIGN.md#5-spaceship-hub).

- **The colony's hub:** Waypost Ostra is an orbital station where the colony's people, its council and its supply depot are; the colony's settlements on the surface are outposts (later). The players supply it, and when the colony has to leave at the end, it leaves from here. A second station comes later with the story (the lost expedition's abandoned base, deeper in, e.g. in orbit around Gaulm); inhabited outposts (handcrafted landing sites without enemies) later still.
- **Why players go there:** the story and quest givers outside the crew (the council, other prospecting crews, colonists), crew members who join (some are met there first, see [`crew.md`](crew.md)), the ore exchange, deliveries to the colony (see [`quests.md`](quests.md)), and walking around together in co-op between runs. It never gates progress (the heat shield does) and is reachable from the start, without a tier.
- **Docking:** the navigator picks the station like a region; the flight ends without clouds: the station grows in the windows and docking clamps take hold (about as long as a flight to another body, see [`opening-planets.md`](opening-planets.md)). The ramp then leads (to the right, as always) into the station's hangar. No timer, no storm, no mission: the ramp button lowers and raises the ramp freely; weapons stay holstered. Leaving: pick a destination at the navigator and set course, the ship undocks. Where the ship is gets saved (the station counts as a place like a body); NPC topics and story flags are in the ship save already.
- **The map:** handcrafted and compact (the far end about a minute's walk from the ramp), an interior like the ship, so the ship's camera rules apply (roof and camera-side walls cut away, low stubs, floor signs). One deck around a big central hall: the **hangar** (the ship docks here), the **concourse** (the hall: colonists, stalls, later the quest notice board), the **supply depot** (deliveries, the ore exchange), the **council office** (the story's quest givers), a **bar** (other crews, rumors, crew to meet) and an **observation gallery** (big windows onto the planet below, story moments later). Greybox rooms from data first, like the ship.
- **The ore exchange,** a safety valve at a bad rate: any common or mid ore into another common or mid ore, 3 for 1. Signature ores can be traded away but never bought, so a planet's signature ore still has to be mined there. An object, not a menu: a counter at the depot with hoppers, whose card picks what goes in and what comes out (amounts in steps), paid from the player's own hold like the upgrades.
- **No shops:** ores are the only currency and the ship has the upgrade stations, so stalls and vendors are scenery and story. Instead, station NPCs **ask for things**: requests to bring an amount of an ore or a refined alloy, handed in at them like any quest delivery (see [`quests.md`](quests.md)).
- **Who's there:** named NPCs (with the crew's NPC template: name plate, topics, quests): the depot master **Kade** (exchange, deliveries), the council member **Selin** (story), the rival prospector **Rook** (banter, later quests) and the bartender **Nell** (rumors, hints about regions and set pieces); their stories in [`story.md`](story.md); each explains what their place does, and none mentions locked content. A handful of ambient colonists walk set routes or stand at stalls, without talking (a name plate and a short line up close at most), cheap enough for VR.
- **Co-op:** the station is part of the host's session like the ship: guests can join while docked and appear aboard or in the hangar. No public lobby with strangers.
- **Look:** the same industrial, weathered human tech as the ship, but lived-in and crowded: cargo containers, cables, patched hull plates, warm work lights against the dark, colonists in worn suits; big windows show the planet below and space. Concept images first (ComfyUI), then greybox.
