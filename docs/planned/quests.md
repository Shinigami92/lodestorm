# Quests (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 16 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. Built so far (A1–A3): quests as data, offering with the diamond marker, accepting, hand-ins, the goals deliver, mine and build, the quest board's quests, jobs and messages, tracking, the story director and the calls from stations with their diamond on the navigator, the goals reach a site and clear nests with the quest sites and quest nests they put into a region's visits (see [Quests](../design/missions.md#quests) and Between missions in [Story](../design/story.md)); the rest below is still to come. Crates hold supply caches, no fragments yet.

## How quests work

- **Built from data:** a quest has its giver, its kind (main, side, request), what it needs to be offered (story flags, an opened body, reputation), its **steps** and its rewards. A step has one or more **goals** and ends with a **hand-in** at the giver or at another NPC it names. Each kind of goal is a Resource of its own, so a new kind is code once and data after. Its lines (the offer, a goal done, the hand-in) use the dialogue Resources and are voiced as the NPC who says them (the quest's type joins `VoiceLines.SPEAKING` with a `spoken_lines()`, see [`technical/voice.md`](../technical/voice.md)); the Dialogue Manager addon gets a second look before the story writing starts.
- **Eight kinds of goals:** **deliver** an amount of an ore (filled from the hauls while the step is active, or paid from the hold of whoever hands it in) or an alloy (from their rack), see [`coop-ore.md`](coop-ore.md); **clear nests** in a region (the step's own quest nests, see Quest nests and hunts); **recover fragments** from a region's crates; **recover an item** at a quest site; **reach a site** (find the expedition's camp, scan a wreck: walk into it); **hunt** a strain in a region (e.g. five spitters, from a brood nest at the step's site) or the Ravager (in its lair at the site, see [`aliens.md`](aliens.md)); **mine** an amount of an ore in one run (brought aboard); **build** something aboard (a weapon from its blueprint, a crafted miner, an upgrade level), which teaches the stations.
- **Offering and accepting:** while an NPC has a quest to offer, their amber marker is a **diamond** (the round one stays for new topics). The quest is a topic in their list ("About that job..."); its card shows what it asks, where, and the rewards, with **Accept** and **Not now** (it stays offered, nothing is lost). No limit on active quests.
- **Quest sites:** while a step is active, it puts what it needs into its region's next visits: a quest site (the map part its goal names, e.g. the expedition's camp or a wreck, placed like a set piece in an area that fits; what the goal needs sits at the part's quest anchor), fragments into the crates, its quest nests, a brood nest or a lair. They go away once the step is done; the region's own set pieces stay. New sites are map parts, so data.
- **What counts:** a run's progress counts once someone gets back aboard (a lost run counts nothing); carried quest items once their carrier is aboard. Kills and cleared nests count for the whole group. A quest never fails; a step stays open until it's done.
- **Rewards** show on the offer card before accepting, as badges: ores or alloys, a blueprint, a furnace (see [`refinery.md`](refinery.md)), a crew member joining (see [`crew.md`](crew.md)), reputation, new topics or the next quest. No experience points, no random loot.
- **Reputation:** five pips per NPC, on their name plate. Quests raise it; it opens their later chains and personal topics, nothing else (no prices), and it never drops.

## Quest nests and hunts

Decided 2026-10-30.

- **Looks of their own:** the nests a quest puts into a region are told apart by their shape from the camera, not only by being bigger, and by a glow of their own (not amber, which stays the quests' marker). Two kinds besides the ordinary nest, so three silhouettes: the **quest nest** and the **brood nest**. Greybox shapes first (variants of the nest's model), finished in the art pass.
- **Quest nests (clear nests):** while the step is active, every visit to its region places its own group of quest nests, as many as are still to destroy, in the areas far enough from the ship (like the ordinary ones, which stay); only these count. They hatch like ordinary nests. Each one destroyed counts once someone is back aboard, so the next visit places only the ones left.
- **Brood nest (hunt a strain):** the hunt's site (a map part) holds a brood nest. Once a player comes close it hatches the hunted strain in packs, until the hunt's count is reached; only kills of that strain from it count. The step also needs the brood nest destroyed (a small boss fight). Kills and the nest count once someone is back aboard; while some are missing, the next visit places the brood nest again.
- **The Ravager's lair (hunt the Ravager):** a lair at the site (a map part) where the Ravager sleeps until a player comes close; killing it does the job. Only on bodies where it lives (tier 3 and on).

## Blueprint fragments and quest items

A quest step can ask for the fragments of a blueprint in a region (e.g. "recover the three fragments in Redscar Flats"). Only while that step is active do the region's crates hold them, one per crate, so it takes one run or several; there are no fragments outside quests (otherwise a crate holds the supply cache, as today). A fragment is carried like ore (lost when its carrier dies, and found again in a later crate) and counts once it's aboard. Handing the step in at the quest giver puts the fragments together and opens the next step, e.g. the blueprint's unlock. Quest items belong to their quest (shown on the back while carried and on the quest board), so there is no inventory and no trading; bringing something to a station is a hand-in at an NPC there.

## What's told

- **The main story:** one main chain in six acts, one per planet (the moons inside their planet's act), each with 4–6 steps, along the lost expedition's trail. Each act ends at the next heat shield level (the story wraps it, but the heat shield can always be installed without it, see Opening planets in [Missions](../design/missions.md); the one exception: the MK2 plate for Vehra waits for the whole crew aboard, see [`crew.md`](crew.md)) and gives the prototype blueprints (see [`weapons.md`](weapons.md)). One main step is always active; Selin (the council member at Waypost Ostra) and the pilot carry it. The acts' outline: see [`story.md`](story.md).
- **Side chains:** each crew member and station NPC has 2–3 chains over the campaign (about 25–35 side quests besides the requests): their posts' blueprints (the gunsmith's colony arms, later the miner mechanic's drill heads; the furnaces and the miners' cradles come through the main quest, see [`balance.md`](balance.md)), crew joining, and their own story. Some steps have a bonus goal (e.g. "also clear the third nest") that pays a little extra.
- **Requests:** NPCs (on the station above all, where there are no shops, see [`station.md`](station.md)) ask for an amount of an ore or a refined alloy, delivered from the hold by a hand-in at them, like any quest step. The depot master always has two open, the other NPCs one now and then; a new one comes after one is handed in. Each pays reputation and another ore or alloy at a better rate than the exchange. Never needed for the story.
- **The expedition's logs:** one per region (25) at the expedition's sites, optional lore, found by walking into the log's site (no quest needed); read again at the quest board (later also at the comms log, M7). The main story never depends on them.

Over the radio (a radio bubble): a short line when landing in a region with an active step and when a step's goal is done ("That's the second nest. Come back to me."); the lines stay with the quest and can be heard again at the giver.

## Co-op

Quests belong to the host's save. Anyone can open a crate and carry a fragment, and it counts for the host's quest once its carrier is aboard. A reward that unlocks gear (a blueprint) goes to every player in the session at the hand-in, into their own save, and so do ores and alloys (like a haul); the rest is the host's (see [`coop-ore.md`](coop-ore.md)). A guest's own quests don't show in someone else's session for now.
