# Saving and statistics

## Saving

What's saved and when: [`design/death-and-persistence.md`](../design/death-and-persistence.md). A `SaveGame` node in the map (`game/save/save_game.gd`) loads the save when the map starts and writes it.

- **Files:** JSON in `save/` under `SaveGame.directory`: `ship.json` for the host's part (the story's flags (`Ship.story`), the heat shield (`Ship.heat_shield`: its MK level, whether the next plate is the project and what's paid into it), the furnaces unlocked (`Ship.furnaces`), the cradles open (`Ship.cradles`), the crew's reactions (`Ship.reactions`) and the body the ship is at (`Voyage.at_body`, by its `CelestialBody.id`); later crew and quests) and `player.json` for the local player's (their health, their hold (`Player.hold`, a `PlayerHold`: units per item and the items ever in it), their batches in the furnaces (`Player.refining`: per batch its slot, alloy, bars, bars done and start time in Unix seconds; bars finished since are put into the hold on loading), owned weapons and blueprints, loadout, upgrade levels, skill ranks (each with the levels or ranks under way and what was paid for them), crafted miners (`Player.fleet`: per miner its number, whether it goes along, its parts' levels and levels under way, its build while it's built; where the lost ones were left), the one-time hints seen (`Player.hints`)), split for co-op (see [Co-op progression](../design/progression.md#co-op-progression)). The settings are in `settings.cfg` (`ConfigFile`, `game/save/game_settings.gd`: the language, and the suit ring's size and "Always show" in static vars read every frame, `ring_scale`) next to `save/`, so resetting keeps them.
- **Where:** the game's user data folder (`user://`, see [Engine settings](engine.md)); runs from the editor use `user://dev/`, so the debug overlay's shortcuts never reach a real save. The tests never touch either: `tests/support/pre_run.gd` sets `SaveGame.directory` to empty (nothing is loaded or saved), and `tests/save/test_save_game.gd` uses its own folder.
- **When:** on every change of the hold, the story, the arsenal, the loadout, the upgrades, the skills, the miners or the hints (`changed`, batched to one write per frame) unless a mission is underway (`Mission.is_underway`), and when a mission is returned or lost.
- **Format:** every saved system writes and reads its own part: `save_data()` returns JSON values, `load_data(data)` reads them back and returns whether all of it read (null: nothing saved, nothing changes); the parts that read others take them as an argument (`Loadout.load_data(data, arsenal)` keeps only owned weapons, `Upgrades.load_data(data, only)` only a miner's parts). `SaveGame.ship_data` and `player_data` put the parts together under one key each, `_apply_ship` and `_apply_player` hand them back in order (the voyage after the heat shield, health after the upgrades and skills, then the jobs done since take effect). `SaveData` (`game/save/save_data.gd`) has what they share: resources (ore and alloy types, weapons, upgrades, skills) are saved by their UID (`uid://...`, `SaveData.uid_of`), so they can be moved and renamed; `load_uid` takes only a UID of a resource of the expected type. Each file has a `version` (`SaveGame.VERSION`): raise it when the format changes. Before the release, older saves aren't converted: a file of another version, like one that can't be parsed, is kept as `<file>.corrupt` and the game starts from the defaults. Entries that don't read are skipped with a warning (`SaveData.skipped`); weapons are never taken away and the locker never empties the loadout, so a save without a known (owned) weapon keeps the default arsenal (loadout) instead of leaving the player unarmed. A save that needed repairs is written again right after loading. Files are written to `<file>.tmp` first and then renamed, so a crash never leaves half a save.
- **Reset:** `SaveGame.reset_progress` deletes `save/` and reloads the map, only while `SaveGame.is_solo` (no connected peers and at most one player; hosting a session alone counts, since friends can join any time); the settings terminal disables its button otherwise.
- **Language:** the saved one is applied when the map enters the tree, unless the command line forced another one (`--language`; detected as a locale other than the system's, so forcing the system language doesn't win).

## Statistics

The game records what the local player does, for playtesting and tuning now and for a statistics station and achievements later (see [`planned/stats.md`](../planned/stats.md)).

- **Events:** `GameEvents` (`game/stats/game_events.gd`) declares every event as a typed signal on one bus, `GameEvents.bus` (a static instance, not an autoload, so scripts that run before the autoloads can emit too). Gameplay emits where things happen (`GameEvents.bus.alien_killed.emit(player, weapon_type, alien)`), never waits for a listener, and keeps its own local signals for its own wiring. Events of a player name the one they're credited to; every machine sees all of them and each keeps its own player's. Credit: a `Hit` carries its `weapon_type` (projectiles, blasts, thrown aliens and burning pass it on), an alien's death goes to the player and weapon of the damage that killed it (none for acid, hazards or a bloater bursting), a nest's to the last damage it took. Damage to a player names its `cause` (the alien, the acid, the hazard, the mission for the storm).
- **Recorder:** a `StatsRecorder` node in the map (before `SaveGame`, so what finishes while loading is counted) listens to the bus. It counts into `StatCounters` (a counter by name, split by its labels in a fixed order) over all sessions and over the current run, and writes, in `stats/` under `SaveGame.directory` (so editor runs use `user://dev/stats/`, the tests nothing):
  - `totals.json`: `version`, `updated` and the `counters` over all sessions; written every 30 s when changed, when a run ends and when the game quits (through `<file>.tmp`, like the saves). Resetting the progress keeps it; a file that can't be read is kept as `.corrupt` and the counters start over.
  - `sessions/<date>_<time>.jsonl`: one JSON object per line, `t` (seconds into the session), `e` (the event), `run` and `run_t` while a run is underway, and the event's fields. Frequent events (attacks, hits, damage dealt, healing, ore arriving, ammo paid) are only counted; `mission_started` has the brief (body, region, biome, difficulty, length, seed, tier, duration) and the gear (loadout, upgrade levels, skill ranks, maximum health), `mission_ended` the result, the run's seconds and its counters. Damage taken is logged once per cause and second (amount and hits). The newest 20 sessions are kept. Written through a buffer flushed every 5 s and when a run ends.
- **Ids:** resources and scenes are named by their path in `game/` without the extension (`weapons/types/harl/harl`, `aliens/strains/brute/brute`, `stations/upgrade/upgrade_station`), never by a display name, so names can change; a moved file starts new counters. Damage causes besides aliens are the class in snake case (`acid_puddle`, `ground_vent`), the storm `storm`.
- **Counters** (labels in order): `attacks` (weapon), `hits` and `hits_armored` (weapon, strain), `damage_dealt` and `kills` (weapon, strain), `aliens_appeared` (strain), `damage_dealt_nests` (weapon), `nests_destroyed` (weapon, or `others`), `reloads` (weapon, `reload`/`vent`), `reload_presses` (weapon, `hit`/`miss`), `reloads_denied` (weapon), `ore_for_ammo` (ore), `damage_blocked` (weapon), `dashes`, `damage_taken` and `deaths` (cause), `healed`, `respawns`, `miners_placed`, `miners_picked_up`, `miners_broken`, `miners_repaired`, `miners_ran_dry` (the player's miners), `miners_built`, `miner_parts_finished` (upgrade), `miners_lost`, `ore_collected`, `ore_hauled` and `ore_lost` (ore), `weapons_unlocked` and `blueprints_learned` (weapon), `upgrades_finished` (upgrade), `skills_finished` (skill), `bars_refined` (alloy), `heat_shield_plates`, `story_flags`, `missions_started` (tier), `missions_ended` (`returned`/`lost`), `storms`, `menus_opened` (menu), `seconds` and `meters` (`aboard`, `outside`, `menu`, `dead`; meters only walking), `sessions`.
- **Adding an event:** a signal in `GameEvents`, its emit where it happens, and in `StatsRecorder` a handler that counts it (and logs it unless it's frequent); `tests/stats/test_stats_recorder.gd` checks the recorder.
