# The crew (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 13 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. The eight crew members stand at their posts with their first topics and their own models, call in over the radio when something happens (see [NPCs](../design/ship.md)); what's left:

- **Crew joining** (decided 2026-10-27, with the quests, see [`quests.md`](quests.md); who they are: see [`characters.md`](characters.md)), values **TBD (playtest)**; the rules are built (16 C3a, see [Crew joining](../design/crew.md)):
  - **Before they join:** Dace at a stall in Ostra's concourse, at a workbench (the stall's colonist mechanic moves to another stall); Wren in Ostra's hangar, at a suit workbench by the side door to the depot (new furniture); Fenna in the bar, at Rook's booth with his crew; Hollis in Hollow Mire at his camp (a quest site with his old miner), while his step is active. Each has topics of their own before joining (who they are, what they'd do aboard), from the same profile.
  - **Joining at Ostra:** after the hand-in that makes them join, they walk aboard: from their place to the hangar, up the ramp, through the ship to their post (from the station's paths to the ship's at the ramp). Leaving waits for them like for players ("Wait until Dace is aboard"); their place at Ostra stays empty afterwards (the stall closed).
  - **Hollis out there,** the first person in a generated location and the template for later ones (outposts, Rook's distress call): aliens ignore him (nothing fights people yet), he stays at his camp; once his step's goal is done (his miner freed: its nest cleared and a part brought), he walks to the ship along the passes and goes aboard. A lost run counts nothing, so he's at his camp again on the next visit.
  - **Until they join** (the rest of it; built in C3a: not at their post, a stand-in topic, see [Crew joining](../design/crew.md)): their room's work light off and their cabin empty, once rooms have their own work lights and the cabins their owners' things.
  - **The join quests** (round 1 decided 2026-10-31; their lines are round 2, amounts **TBD (playtest)**), each with a different kind of goal, none gating anything but Vehra:
    - **Dace, "Real ore":** offered by Dace at his stall once Brannik's colony arms chain is done (the Brask blueprint is in hand, and somebody has to build it); deliver the ore for his first real part (e.g. 80 Kyrite and 20 Meridine); handed in at Dace, he joins.
    - **Wren, "Out there":** she asks herself, offered once she's been talked to; recover the fragments of an old survey suit from the crates of Cinder Steps (she wants a suit of her own for out there); handed in at Wren, she joins.
    - **Hollis, "Overdue":** offered by **Kade** (an old prospector in Hollow Mire hasn't come in for his supplies); reach his camp and talk to him (his miner is stuck, a nest grew around it), then clear the nest at his miner and bring him a part from the region's crates; handed in at Hollis at his camp, he walks to the ship and comes aboard. Leaving waits until he's aboard ("Wait until Hollis is aboard"); he walks at a jog.
    - **Hollis' old miner** comes aboard with him as a prop in the cargo bay beside the cradles (his project, his later tinkering), not an extra miner: miners come from the cradles, and those come through the main quest (see [`balance.md`](balance.md)).
    - **Fenna, "Rook's leftovers":** offered by Fenna at Rook's booth once Dace, Wren and Hollis are aboard (the end of act 1); recover her galley crate at a quest site on Rimeback (Lisk), left in a cache when Rook's crew turned back; handed in at Fenna, she joins.
  - **Their topics before joining:** two each, "About <name>" (who they are, at their place) and one about coming aboard, which turns into the quest offer once it's offered; after joining, their station topics as now.
  - **What stays at Ostra:** Dace's stall with its shutter down and his sign still up, Wren's bench with her tools left on it, Rook's booth one person less.
  - **Voices:** the new lines with the existing voices of Dace, Wren, Hollis and Fenna (and Kade's and Brannik's for their few).
  - **Built in four PRs:** **C3a** the rules (join flags, the crew not aboard yet, stand-in topics, gated lines, the turns, Vehra's card, the reward, the tools), **C3b** waiting at Ostra and walking aboard, **C3c** Hollis out there, **C3d** the four join quests with their lines and voices.
- **More transmissions:** each new event is lines in the profiles of those it concerns plus one hook (e.g. the first nest destroyed, the first alloy, the apex killed, a crew member joining); during a run only quest steps and story moments (with the quests), mostly from the pilot.
- **Between missions** (decided 2026-10-27, see Between missions in [`story.md`](story.md)): personal arcs with a beat in every act, opinions about each other, overheard talks among themselves, the heat shield plate's beats, a gathering at the mess table at each act's end, Brannik's range challenges and Fenna's dish per planet, small incidents aboard; nobody of the crew dies.
- **Ilse's topic about falling** says what happens in the world (see [`world.md`](world.md)): the suit's stasis shell, its beacon, the retrieval drone and the dumped ore.
- **Co-op:** the crew is the host's, like the ship: anyone can talk to them; what's been heard is the host's story flags, the transmissions' turns are saved with the ship.
