# The crew (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 13 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. The eight crew members stand at their posts with their first topics and greybox accessories, call in over the radio when something happens (see [NPCs](../GAME_DESIGN.md#5-spaceship-hub)); what's left:

- **Crew joining** (with the quests, see [`quests.md`](quests.md)): stations keep working without crew, so nothing gates progress behind finding someone; joining adds the person: their topics, explanations, quest chains and reputation. Aboard from the start are Brannik, Tamsin, Okoro and Ilse (the ship can't fly, fight or heal without them); Dace, Wren and Hollis join early (Torvane and Waypost Ostra, first quests), Fenna later at Ostra. Until the quests exist, everyone is aboard at their post. Story topics and quests come with the quest framework.
- **More transmissions:** each new event is lines in the profiles of those it concerns plus one hook (e.g. the first nest destroyed, the first alloy, the apex killed, a crew member joining); during a run only quest steps and story moments (with the quests), mostly from the pilot.
- **Their looks** (decided 2026-10-26): shipboard work clothes of their own, no armored suit (only the players wear one) and no uniform, worn and practical; shared by everyone: a thin glowing collar band in their own color and a small crew patch with the game's emblem on the left shoulder; each one's accessory from the greybox is part of their clothes. Brannik, early sixties, broad, clean-shaven, a padded armorer's vest with magazine pouches; Tamsin, late thirties, auburn bun, a navy flight jacket and a headset; Okoro, about fifty, tall, shaved head, rust-brown coveralls tied at the waist and a welding visor; Ilse, mid-fifties, ash-blond knot, a pale medical tunic with a cross on each shoulder; Dace, mid-thirties, wiry, a scorched leather apron and goggles; Wren, early twenties, black hair with teal tips, a light-grey technician jumpsuit and a display on her forearm; Hollis, late sixties, long gray hair tied back, a canvas jacket and a hard hat with a lamp; Fenna, mid-forties, a Black woman with curly hair under a cap, a cook's jacket and a long apron. Four women and four men, as the German role words have it. Their concepts are rated (batch 10, one picked per person); their own models come with the art pass (see [`people.md`](people.md); replacing the darker suit and the greybox accessories).
- **Co-op:** the crew is the host's, like the ship: anyone can talk to them; what's been heard is the host's story flags, the transmissions' turns are saved with the ship.
