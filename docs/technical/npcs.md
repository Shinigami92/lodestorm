# NPCs

- **Crew (NPCs):** `Npc` (`game/npcs/npc.gd`, `game/npcs/npc.tscn`) is a station: the person is its one part, its card a `TalkCard`, and `Bubble` a second transparent `WorldPanel` with a `SpeechBubble` (`game/npcs/speech_bubble.tscn`: the line in a panel with a tail drawn below its middle, kept to the bottom of its screen; the line is a `RichTextLabel` whose names `Keywords.highlight` colors, `game/ui/keywords.gd`: it reads every name from the star system (a body also by its `short_name`, "Ostra"), `all_weapons.tres`, the ore and alloy catalogs and every named `NpcProfile` under `game/npcs/`, and matches them as whole words in the shown language, plus a German genitive "s"; `RadioBubble` does the same) that faces the camera above the head while they talk (`NpcBody.get_head_position`). Their `Body` is an `NpcBody` (`game/npcs/npc_body.tscn`: the model, the animator, the collision, the name plate), which the station's ambient colonists use on their own (`Colonist`, `game/npcs/colonist.tscn`: a body, a route of waypoints in its parent's space, a remark bubble placed by `SpeechBubble.place` and scaled with the camera rig's view; each colonist has a profile of its own in `game/npcs/ostra/colonists/`, with a role and `remarks`; `seated` sits where it's placed for good, set at once with `NpcBody.settle()`; `seat_stop`, a waypoint of its route where it sits for `sit_time` instead of the short stop, turning to `seat_yaw`, and gets up before walking on; a seat's spot is where the feet are, the hips `SIT_BACK` behind it on a seat `SEAT_HEIGHT` high, both in `tools/blender/humanoid.py`, and `game/space_station/ostra/ostra_build.py` places the seated colonists and its benches by them): it dresses itself from its `profile` (times its own `tint`), turns towards whom it `faces` (the `Npc` sets the player talking; else the nearest player within `NOTICE_RANGE`; not while `sitting`: `NpcAnimator` blends into the model's `Sit` over `sit_blend_time`, a model without it stays standing, and `get_head_position` and the plate go down as far as the skeleton's hips are below their rest), walks with `walk(speed)` and shows the name plate within `PLATE_RANGE` while `plate_allowed` and that player uses no menu. A `Topic` with `requires_body` (a `CelestialBody.id`) only shows once the ship reaches that body (`Npc.get_reached_bodies`). The talk card's list scrolls past `TalkCard.MAX_ROWS`. Who they are is data: an `NpcProfile` (`game/npcs/npc_profile.gd`; the `id` their topics' ids and later quests and reputation use, never changed once released, name, translated role, `accent_color` for the collar light, `suit_tint` multiplying the suit's colors, crew `cabin`, `dialogue`), e.g. `game/npcs/crew/quartermaster.tres` with its `Dialogue` and `Topic`s built in. Crew members walk aboard (their `routine`, an `NpcRoutine` in `game/npcs/npc_routine.gd`, each physics frame): their post (`get_post`) is their spot (where the scene places them, kept in their parent's space: a station's people stay on it while it glides in, and only people aboard walk over the ship's navigation), the `WorkSpot` they're at (`game/npcs/work_spot.gd`: a `Marker3D` in their room, their feet and facing, with the `activities` that fit there; `Npc.work_spots` lists theirs, `move_on` picks another of them or their own spot every `MOVE_ON_TIME` s by their `weight` (their own spot's 1), never the one they're at and one outside their own room only once their greeting is heard; away from their own spot and on the way they're used at their feet within `NpcRoutine.USE_RANGE`, unless the spot is `use_like_home`, e.g. along Nell's counter), or with a `flight_seat` (a `Marker3D` at the seat's spot, facing its way, e.g. the bridge's `PilotSeatSpot` in front of the cockpit's `PilotSeat`) the seat from the moment a destination is chosen (`Voyage.destination`, not yet the `brief` the ship flies to or stands at) or `ShipFlight.is_flying()` until they've sat `NpcRoutine.SEAT_LINGER` s after both. They know where they are: `NpcRoutine.get_room` (the node name of the room they stand in, kept through doorways), `get_own_room` (their own spot's) and `is_away` (`NpcTalk.get_topic_lines` then gives a topic's `away_lines`), from the `RoomPlan` (`game/world/room_plan.gd`: each room's areas as boxes from floor to ceiling between the walls' centers, `room_at(point, keep)` keeping the room before within `KEEP_MARGIN` m) of the map above them (`Ship.room_plan` in the hull, `SpaceStation.room_plan`), which the builders write from the layout (`hull_build.py`'s `room_plan`, `ostra_build.py`'s `ROOMS`); `tests/npcs/test_npc_places.gd` asks for `away_lines` in every topic of a named NPC with a line that points at the room (`PLACE_WORDS`; Ostra's greetings aside, heard before anyone leaves). `NpcWalker` (`game/npcs/npc_walker.gd`) walks them there over the navigation of the map they're on (`DeckNavigation`, `game/world/deck_navigation.gd`: `Ship.navigation`, and `SpaceStation.navigation` with its `bake_box` and `floors` from its `RoomPlan`; a navigation mesh baked on a worker thread the first time anyone asks for a way, from its parent's static colliders inside `bake_box` (the ship's `SHIP_BAKE_BOX`), cell 0.125 m, agent radius 0.375 m, on its own navigation map so it never joins a location's or another map's, following its parent when it moves (a station gliding in); only polygons on the decks' `floors` are kept, not the tops of tables and consoles; about 15 ms of parsing on the main thread and 80 ms of baking aboard), the last step straight from the mesh to the goal (a seat's spot lies just off it), turning into the way they walk, in `ShipDoor.OPENERS` while walking; at the post they turn to its way and, at a seat, sit down (`NpcBody.sitting`; the model needs a `Sit` clip: Tamsin's has one), elsewhere they may only play what fits (`NpcBody.allow_activities`, `NpcAnimator.allowed`: a work spot's `activities`, at their own spot `Npc.home_activities` or else `NpcRoutine.HOME_ACTIVITIES` with work spots, everything their profile has without). On the way to the seat they walk at `NpcRoutine.HURRY_SPEED` and stop for nobody, elsewhere at `WALK_SPEED`, standing while a player is within `NpcBody.NOTICE_RANGE`; while someone talks to them they stay put. `NpcBody` doesn't turn to players while walking. A goal on the other deck takes the lobby's lift (`NpcWalker.LIFT`, `NpcWalker.Ride`): to the gate (`Lift.gate_floor`), `Lift.call_to` while nobody rides it, stepping on while `Lift.boarding` holds it (players on it don't send it off meanwhile), `Lift.send`, riding with the feet on `Lift.platform_floor`, set on the deck's floor (`DeckNavigation.floors`) on arrival and off through the gate; `stop` never strands them on the platform. **Tours:** an `NpcProfile.tour` (`game/npcs/tour.gd`, built into the profile: `start`, the marker they wait at on a new game; the `offer`, `invite`, `resume` and `abort` topics; `stops`, `TourStop` resources with the `id` of a marker under the ship's `Tour` node and their `lines`) runs as an `NpcTour` (`game/npcs/npc_tour.gd`, `NpcRoutine.tour`): `NpcRoutine.is_waiting` (no tour running, the offer not heard) puts them at the start (placed there on the first physics frame: the save is read by then), `Npc.get_greeting` makes the offer the greeting instead of the dialogue's greeting there, `get_choices` adds the invite once open and offers only `resume`/`abort` during a tour, and `NpcTalk._finish_topic` starts, goes on with or ends it and closes the talk (deferred). At a stop, `NpcTour.at_stop` waits for the `leader` within `REACH`, then says each line with `Npc.say_aside` (the bubble above their head and their voice, placed and scaled like a colonist's remark, the plate hidden; returns how long it stays) and moves on; `hush` starts a stop over after a talk, `is_lost` ends it after `AWAY_TIME` s beyond `AWAY_RANGE`. `tools/translations.gd` collects `TourStop.lines`; `NpcProfile.spoken_lines` lists the tour's lines with the rest for the voices. A `Dialogue` has a `greeting` (played by itself while it isn't heard), `prompts` (the bubble while the list shows: one per talk, `pick_prompt`, never the one before; `DEFAULT_PROMPT` without any) and `topics`; a `Topic` has a stable `id`, a `title`, `lines` (one bubble each), `guest_lines` (what a co-op guest hears instead, `lines_for`, `Npc.is_guest`), `away_lines` (what they say instead outside their own room, the lines that fit anywhere repeated; a guest's come first), the story flags it `requires` and optionally a `requires_weapon` (only shown to a player who owns it); `is_open(listener, story)` decides, so topics with more conditions (quests, reputation) override it. Texts are the English originals like everywhere (`tools/translations.gd` collects the role, prompts, titles and every line). Hearing a topic to the end sets its `id` in the ship's `Story` (`game/story/story.gd`: a set of flags, saved with the ship; later quest steps and crew joining go there too). Flags are saved by name, so their names follow one scheme (`Story.is_valid_flag`: lowercase parts joined by dots, `set_flag` refuses others with an error): `<npc>.<topic>` (`<npc>` the profile's `id`), `quest.<quest>.<step>`, `reward.<quest>`, `crew.<npc>.joined`, `event.<event>` (a one-time event, e.g. `event.first_death`, `event.landed.<body>`); `tests/story/test_story.gd` checks the crew's topic ids. What one player sees for themselves (a one-time hint) isn't a story flag but theirs: `Player.hints` (`PlayerHints`, saved with the player; `RampHint` uses it); `get_choices(listener)` lists the topics open for a player (the one talking by default), `has_news(listener)` drives the amber marker (for the nearest player). While open, the talk is `Npc.talk` (an `NpcTalk`, `game/npcs/npc_talk.gd`: `open` and `greet` as it opens, `close`; the `Npc` keeps what they can talk about, their voice, `say` and `say_aside`): `step` moves `choice` up and down the list (the last entry is "Goodbye"), `press` (the card's `press_primary`) plays the highlighted topic or the next line, `leave_topic` (`Npc._back`) returns to the list. With `GameSettings.dialogue_auto_continue`, `_speak` sets the line's wait (`_line_time`: `VoiceLines.stay_time`, the recording's length plus `AUTO_PAUSE`, else `reading_time` of the shown text, shared with `say_aside` and the radio), `update` counts it up each frame while the player talks and calls `advance` when it's reached, and passes `get_auto_progress` (0..1, -1 when not waiting) to `TalkCard.show_progress` (an amber `ColorRect` along the bottom of the first button, inside its frame). `_place_focus` puts the camera's `view_focus` between them and the player (where the player stands) when the talk starts, a little to the right on screen (`FOCUS_RIGHT`), so the card on their right stays in view. The model is `assets/player/player.glb` with `PlayerAnimator` (`aiming` off: idle; `walks` on: a slow forward pace plays "Walk", authored at `walk_speed`, instead of the slowed-down run clips). Each NPC brings a small spot light, so they read in the dark rooms.
- **Crew transmissions:** a crew member's radio lines are `CrewReaction`s in their profile (`game/npcs/crew_reaction.gd`: an `event`, an optional `subject` such as a body's id, `players` (`ANY`, `SOLO`, `COOP`: worded for one player or several) and `lines`; `NpcProfile.reaction_lines(event, subject, together)` prefers the subject's own, then the fitting wording). The ship's `CrewReactions` (`game/npcs/crew_reactions.gd`, `Ship.reactions`, a child node) listens to `GameEvents` (`mission_ended`, `player_died` + `player_respawned` for the first fall, `heat_shield_raised`, `weapon_unlocked`, `ore_hauled` for the big haul, `storm_warned`, which `Mission` emits when the timer crosses `warning_time()`) and the ship's `player_left` (the first landing), and `transmit(event, subject, to)`s: the crew member whose turn it is (`turns` per event) among those with lines says their next line (`next_line`, `said` per `<npc>.<event>`) to `to` alone or to every living player (only those outside for the storm warning), worded `together` when there's more than one player and no `to`; one-time events set an `event.*` story flag first. `Player.radio` (`PlayerRadio`, `game/player/player_radio.gd`) `hear`s a call: it shows it in a transparent `WorldPanel` with a `RadioBubble` (`game/npcs/radio_bubble.tscn`: antenna, "<name> · Radio" in the caller's `accent_color`, the line, the panel shrunk to the line's width) left of the player's head, scaled by `SpeechBubble.view_scale` (shared with `Colonist`), for its `VoiceLines.stay_time`, and plays the recording for the local player on the `Radio` bus (`game/audio/bus_layout.tres`: a band-pass at 1.8 kHz and a light distortion, sending to Voice); calls meanwhile queue (`GAP` apart, a newer one about the same topic replacing a waiting one; `clear()` drops them). `get_data`/`restore` save `said`, `turns` and `biggest_haul` in `ship.json` (`reactions`). The debug overlay's "Crew" group calls in about every event (`transmit_all`) and the storm; `tests/npcs/test_crew_reactions.gd` covers it, `test_speech_fit` fits every call in every language. An NPC with only an English anchor speaks English in every voice language (`VOICED_IN_ENGLISH` in the test).
