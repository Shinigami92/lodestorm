# The crew (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 9 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. Today one crew member is aboard, Brannik the quartermaster, with the NPC template (see [NPCs](../GAME_DESIGN.md#5-spaceship-hub)).

## Eight crew members

One per crew cabin, each at a post, explaining what happens there. No captain: the players are the crew's prospectors and run the ship. Names are invented and untranslated, each with its own first letter; each profile sets the character's pronouns and German role word.

| Name | Role | Post | Explains |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brannik** | quartermaster | armory, shooting range | ammo, spares, reloads, the loadout, the range (as today) |
| **Tamsin** | pilot | bridge (the cockpit during flights) | the navigator, the campaign map, flights; the voice on the radio during runs |
| **Okoro** | engineer | engine room, cargo bay | the heat shield and opening planets, the hold; later the refinery |
| **Ilse** | medic | medical bay | health carrying over, healing, respawning; later the rescue drone |
| **Dace** | gunsmith | weapons room | weapon upgrades, blueprints, building weapons |
| **Wren** | suit tech | armor room, skills room | the armor and the suit's systems (the skills) |
| **Hollis** | miner mechanic | miners' room | miners and their upgrades; later crafted miners and drills |
| **Fenna** | cook | mess hall | no station: rumors, hints about regions and set pieces, story glue |

- **Stations work without crew:** every station works from the start, so nothing gates progress behind finding someone. Crew joining (with the quests, see [`quests.md`](quests.md)) adds the person: their topics, explanations, quest chains and reputation. Later, aboard from the start are Brannik, Tamsin, Okoro and Ilse (the ship can't fly, fight or heal without them); Dace, Wren and Hollis join early (Torvane and Waypost Ostra, first quests), Fenna later at Ostra. Until the quests exist, everyone is aboard at their post.
- **What they say:** each has a greeting, 2–4 topics about their post's mechanics (gated by flags, never about locked content) and one about themselves (a hint of their personal thread, see [`story.md`](story.md)); story topics and quests come with the quest framework. Lines follow the dialogue rules (full plain sentences, natural German).
- **Reactions:** a few one-off lines per crew member for events (the first run won, the first death, a new planet opened, a new weapon built, a big haul), shown as a bubble when a player passes within 4 m, once per event, never blocking.
- **The pilot on the radio:** two lines for now, as a radio bubble near the player: the storm warning ("The storm hits in a minute, get back to the ship.") and the first landing on a new body. More radio with the quests.
- **Telling them apart:** today's greybox look (the players' suit darker, their own collar light) plus one accessory each that reads from the isometric camera: the pilot a headset, the engineer a tool belt and welding visor, the medic a white shoulder cross, the gunsmith an apron and goggles, the suit tech an arm display, the miner mechanic a hard hat with a lamp, the cook an apron and cap. Concept images first, models with the art pass.
- **Where they stand:** at their posts, turning towards players; the pilot moves to the cockpit during a flight. No daily routines for now.
- **Co-op:** the crew is the host's, like the ship: anyone can talk to them; what's been heard and which reactions were shown are the host's story flags.

## Health carries over, the medic heals

Today players are healed for each new run. Instead, health carries over between runs: a player keeps what they came back with. The **med bed** in the medical bay is a station: lying down heals to full in a few seconds (greybox 3 s), for free. The medic explains it, and that dying means waking up there with full health. The collar light shows a player is hurt; the ramp button's card says so once ("Not at full health: the medical bay is at the nose"). Respawning in the medical bay stays at full health.
