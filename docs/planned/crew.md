# The crew (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 13 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. The eight crew members stand at their posts with their first topics and greybox accessories (see [NPCs](../GAME_DESIGN.md#5-spaceship-hub)); what's left:

- **Crew joining** (with the quests, see [`quests.md`](quests.md)): stations keep working without crew, so nothing gates progress behind finding someone; joining adds the person: their topics, explanations, quest chains and reputation. Aboard from the start are Brannik, Tamsin, Okoro and Ilse (the ship can't fly, fight or heal without them); Dace, Wren and Hollis join early (Torvane and Waypost Ostra, first quests), Fenna later at Ostra. Until the quests exist, everyone is aboard at their post. Story topics and quests come with the quest framework.
- **Reactions:** a few one-off lines per crew member for events (the first run won, the first death, a new planet opened, a new weapon built, a big haul), shown as a bubble when a player passes within 4 m, once per event, never blocking.
- **The pilot on the radio:** two lines for now, as a radio bubble near the player: the storm warning ("The storm hits in a minute, get back to the ship.") and the first landing on a new body. More radio with the quests.
- **Their looks:** concept images first, then their own models with the art pass (replacing the darker suit and the greybox accessories).
- **Co-op:** the crew is the host's, like the ship: anyone can talk to them; what's been heard and which reactions were shown are the host's story flags.
