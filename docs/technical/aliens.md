# Aliens' data

- **Aliens' data:** a strain is an `AlienStrain` (`game/aliens/alien_strain.gd`: its body `scene`, health, armor and its arc, stagger threshold and time, knockback, movement, ranges, `cap_share`, and `attacks`, tried in order); `AlienStrain.spawn(role)` hatches one, so nests only refer to strains. Besides its body and movement a strain has its `tier`, its `role` (a nest hatches guards, hunters and raiders from the strains of that role; `weight` picks among the guards' and raiders' strains), `leash` (guards stay within it of their `Alien.home`, where they hatched, and walk back), `keep_away` (backing off, the spitter) and burrowing (`burrows`: under the ground the alien's collision is off, it moves along the ground (a short ray finds it) without physics and stops under rocks and cliffs, `is_hittable()` is false for shots, swings and the target lock, and a `BurrowRipple` shows it). An attack is an `AlienAttack` resource (`game/aliens/attacks/`: damage, reach, cooldown, `wind_up`, `recovery`, whether it's used on miners) whose kind decides when it can start and what it does (`AlienBite`, `AlienPounce`, `AlienSpit` lobbing an `AcidGlob` that leaves an `AcidPuddle`, `AlienEruption` from under the ground, `AlienSlam` hitting every player around a spot ahead; `clip_lead` starts the model's attack clip that long before the hit, for a clip that is the telegraph and moves the legs itself (the warden's rear-up: `AlienLegs` is off until the attack ends); `underground` says from where an attack starts, `approach()` how close the alien comes for it); each strain's attacks are their own files beside it, so the debug overlay can tune them. More kinds: `AlienCharge` (the brute: a `ChargeLine` of decals on the ground during the wind-up, the alien's `attack_facing` held on it, then a running `lunge` that ignores players' collision and hits everyone in the lane; a stall, `lunge_stalled`, dazes it through `stagger_for`), `AlienScream` (boosts every alien around through `AlienStatus.boost`, emits `Alien.called_for_help`, a `ScreamWave` ring) and `AlienBurst` (kills its alien; its `on_death` leaves an `AcidCloud`, an `AcidPuddle` that hurts aliens too). Attacks get hooks: `prepare` (the wind-up starts), `finish` (the attack ends or is broken off) and `on_death`. An attack is shared by every alien of its strain, so what it keeps for one run lives on the alien, in `Alien.attack_state` (dropped when the attack ends, freed with the alien; e.g. `AlienCharge.Run`: its line, length and the players hit). `AlienStrain.armored_stagger` is the share of stagger hits on the armor fill, `AlienStrain.mend` an `AlienMend` (`game/aliens/support/`) for which the alien adds `MendLinks`: strands (a `CylinderMesh` per link with `mend_link.gdshader`, from the tendrils' tip bones) to the most hurt aliens near it, healing them and raising their `AlienStatus.bonus_armor`. `AlienStatus` (`Alien.status`) holds what acts on an alien from outside: the scream's boost, the frenzy's `hive_speed` and a tender's link (kept off `Alien`, which is at gdlint's 20 public methods); while `is_buffed()` the alien's `AlienLook` scales its `Model` up (`BUFFED_SCALE`) and swaps its rim light for a yellow-green one. `Alien` runs the phases (wind-up, act, recovery), the stagger meter and the push, and offers attacks `strike`, `lunge`/`lunge_step`, `stagger_for` and `reach_to`. Weapons hand aliens a `Hit` (`game/weapons/hit.gd`: damage, stagger, push, direction, point, attacker); `Alien.take_hit` applies armor and returns the damage that got through (for the damage readout), `take_damage` is plain damage (burning ground, debug). `HitSpray` (`game/aliens/hit_spray.gd`) is a one-shot `CPUParticles3D` that frees itself. The hive (`game/aliens/hive/hive.tres`, an `AlienHive`) lists every strain and the packs (`AlienPack`: how many of each strain, a weight); `AlienHive.table(tier, biome, preview, seed)` builds a `HatchTable` (from which escalation each strain hatches, the biome's `favored_strains`), which `GeneratedLocation` hands to all its nests (hand-placed nests build their own from the mission's tier). A nest picks packs for hunters and single guards and raiders from it (`HatchTable.pick_single`); hunters and raiders out count by cap share. Every second a nest sums up the working miners whose nearest nest it is (`raider_draw_factor`), which speeds up its raider countdown; `AlienNest.is_frenzied` (from the mission's warning on) sets `status.hive_speed` on its aliens each frame and uses the end values for hunters; it connects every alien it hatches to `called_for_help` and lets the nest nearest to the scream `answer_call` (a pack now, boosted by the scream's values the signal carries, a mouth flare and a `ScreamWave`, then `call_cooldown`). The signal is emitted by `AlienScream` and answered by nests, since nothing a strain loads may reach `AlienNest` (see below). Fliers (`AlienStrain.flight_height` > 0, the darter): `Alien.flight` (`AlienFlight`) turns the horizontal velocity into one that holds the height over the ground (rays on the `WORLD` layer below it and a bit ahead, never below its target's ground), skips the navigation mesh and avoidance and pushes fliers apart; the scene's collision mask is 0, so it bumps into nothing; `AlienDive` (an `AlienCharge` with its line laid on the ground) sets `flight.low` while it acts and recovers; a dead flier drops to the ground. `AlienStrain.cloak` (an `AlienCloak`) adds `Alien.cloaking` (`AlienCloaking`): it swaps the `Creature` mesh's material for `cloak.gdshader` (the screen texture bent along the normals, which writes the stencil like the other bodies, so the occluded silhouette still only shows behind cliffs) while it isn't fully shown, fades `Eyes` and `Glands` through `GeometryInstance3D.transparency`, and turns off its shadow and rim light; `PlayerAim.is_lockable` skips it while `is_hidden()`. `AlienStrain.retreat_time` makes it back off after every attack (`_end_attack`). An attack started while cloaked sets `AlienCloaking.ambush`, which raises the next `Alien.strike` to `AlienCloak.ambush_damage`. `AlienHook` (only once the stalker is fully shown) launches a `FleshHook`: a straight hook with a sinew line (a stretched `CylinderMesh`), a ray per step on `SHOTS` (aliens and players other than prey skipped), that catches a player with `GroundContact.snare` (a timed share of the walking speed, counted down by the player's `tick`; no pull). `AlienStrain.circles` makes an alien between `keep_away` and its reach move around its target (`Alien._chase`), and `AlienAttack.on_the_move` keeps it moving through an attack's wind-up and recovery: the skimmer's `AlienSpit` with `arc_per_meter` 0 is a straight bolt. The apex (`AlienHive.apex`, the Ravager) isn't in `strains`: `GeneratedLocation._add_apex` rolls `AlienHive.apex_chance_at(tier)` with its own RNG from the seed (the rest of the generation stays the same) and adds an `ApexLair` with the route (the middles of reachable areas at least `APEX_LANDING_DISTANCE` from the landing site); the lair spawns it with the mission's tier factors in `_ready`, like nests hatch guards. With `AlienStrain.roams` an alien without a target walks to a random point of `Alien.route` after another; `AlienStrain.tremor` adds `Alien.tremor` (`AlienTremor`), which `AlienLegs.stepped` (a foot landing) and `AlienSlam` thump: `DustPuff`s at the foot and `IsoCameraRig.tremble` on every rig whose target is within `REACH`. `AlienShed` spawns its strain beside the alien as hunters, under the cap read from `alien_nest_settings.tres`. Players' and aliens' meshes are on `RenderLayers.CHARACTERS` (`game/world/render_layers.gd`), and so is what players carry and what floats around them (the weapon, the cargo tubes, the armor pips, the ship pointer, arriving ore, the pause panel; put there where it's built). A `ChargeLine`'s and the `AimReticle`'s decals only reach `RenderLayers.WORLD` and `RenderLayers.INTERIOR` (the ship's shell, which the rim light leaves out), so lanes don't paint them red and the reticle stays on the ground. The suit ring, the miners' rings and the target brackets are on `RenderLayers.MARKINGS`: they're drawn pulled towards the camera (`ground_marking.gdshaderinc`), so a decal reaching them would paint a shifted copy of itself on them; an `Interactable`'s `Highlight` goes there too. The reticle's `normal_fade` (`AimReticle.NORMAL_FADE`) fades it out on steep surfaces instead of smearing it down their sides. Ground markings to read are on `RenderLayers.MARKINGS` too: the stations' stand rings, the rooms' names, the floor guides, a lobbed weapon's landing ring, a throw's sight line, a placed charge's ring. What's light, not a surface, is on `RenderLayers.EFFECTS`: holograms (the holo table's location, the navigator's view, the skill graph, the ghost miners, the outlines of heat shield plates not built yet, holo cards and their leader lines), fields (the shooting range's barrier, Mantle's), weapons' and aliens' effects, the flight's clouds and streaks, and text and plates. Whatever builds such a thing puts it there (`RenderLayers.put` or `layers`); `tests/world/test_render_layers.gd` walks the map and fails on text, sprites, particles, shader or see-through materials left on `WORLD` (except the ship's windows). Hive colors live on `Alien` (`COLOR_GLOW`), not on the nest: a script a strain's scene depends on must not reach `AlienNest`, since through `Mission`, `Voyage` and `GeneratedLocation` (which preloads the nest scene, which refers to the strain) that loops back to the strain while it loads. **Destroyable nests:** a nest's `Body` is a `Destructible` (`game/weapons/destructible.gd`, a `StaticBody3D` in its own group: health, armor, `take_hit`/`take_damage`, `damaged` and `destroyed`), so weapons and the target lock reach nests without referring to `AlienNest` (the player's weapons are in an alien's dependency graph, see above): `Projectile` and `SwingDelivery` hit it besides aliens, `PlayerAim.target` and `Weapon.aim_target` are an `Alien` or a `Destructible` (`PlayerAim.is_lockable`; aliens are tried first) and `DamageNumber.show_hit` takes either. `WeaponTarget` (`game/weapons/weapon_target.gd`) is the one place that tells the two apart (`is_target`, `is_hittable`, `aim_point`, `edge`, `take_hit`, `take_damage`): weapons, fire and the target lock go through it, so a new kind of target only adds itself there. `AlienNest` sets the body's values (`max_health()`: tier and players), sets `AlienStatus.defending` on its guards on a hit (`AlienTargeting.update` takes that player first while within the leash), counts the bursts due from the body's health, warns, `_burst`s and shows the model's `Cracks<stage>` meshes; on `destroyed` it leaves `AlienNest.GROUP` (so draw, screams, surges and the overlay skip the husk), emits its own `destroyed` (for the quests' "clear nests" goal), lets every other nest `surge()` and collapses (model scale, own copies of the mound's and slime's materials, a lower collision cylinder).
