# The people (planned)

Decided 2026-10-27: who the named people are, for the writing rounds (see [`story.md`](story.md), in the frame of [`world.md`](world.md)). Every sheet fits what they already say in the game; a writing round reads the sheets first, and a new person gets a sheet before their lines are written. The twelve named today are all met in act 1; how they look: see [`design/crew.md`](../design/crew.md) and [`design/station.md`](../design/station.md).

Each sheet: **before** (where they come from), **why here**, **wants**, **flaw**, **secret** (what comes out later), **arc** (big beats by act), **ties**, **voice** (how they talk).

## The crew as a whole

- **How it came together:** Brannik and Okoro founded it about half a year before the game starts: Brannik wanted to go inward after his sister (the council has no ship for it, and nobody else would), Okoro wanted to get close to the star. Brannik put in everything he had saved, Okoro knew how to make a ship survive further in. They hired Tamsin (out of work since the haulers landed), Ilse (who had just left the clinic) and the players as their prospectors, the ones who go out there. That's why Ostra greets them as "another prospecting crew": new to the trade, its people from here.
- **The ship** is the survey's old ship, the one that charted the lane 25 years ago, stripped and sold off when the survey ended and bought cheap by Brannik: the reason for the specimen lab and the worn look. Hollis flew on it once ("I know this ship"); its name may be the survey's old one, painted over and painted back in act 1 (the name is open, see [`world.md`](world.md)).
- **Who joins in act 1:** **Hollis** in Hollow Mire, working alone with an old miner after his last crew broke up (a quest: help him get it out, he comes aboard with it); **Dace** at Ostra, a machinist at a stall in the concourse; **Wren** at Ostra, the station's suit fixer, who asks to come; **Fenna** at the end of act 1, at Ostra, after leaving Rook's crew. Until then their rooms are empty and their stations work without them, and Vehra waits until all eight are aboard (see Crew joining in [`crew.md`](crew.md)).
- **Nobody of the crew leaves or dies:** the crew stays the player's home to the end; the one who stays behind is Selin.

## The crew

### Brannik (quartermaster, early sixties)

- **Before:** 30 years as a guard and armorer on the charter's lane ships, never staying anywhere long. Came with the colony 3 years ago because his younger sister **Maren**, a biologist, was on Calder's expedition, there ahead of them.
- **Why here:** the expedition went silent a year ago and the council won't send anyone inward, so he bought a ship.
- **Wants:** to know what happened to Maren. **Flaw:** treats the prospectors like his recruits, overprotective, won't say how much of this is about her. **Secret:** he spent all he had on the ship, and he'd sell it for one clue.
- **Arc:** act 1 his first jobs (his chain, Brask); act 2 the expedition's dropship on Hoarfrost Ridge with Maren's things in it; act 4 at the base, Pell tells him that Maren gave Pell the last working cryo pod; act 5 he brings her notes on the hive to Okoro; act 6 he's the one who says it's time to go.
- **Ties:** Hollis teases him (two old men), protective of Wren, trusts Tamsin's flying blindly.
- **Voice:** short, dry, practical, warmer than he wants to be.

### Tamsin (pilot, late thirties)

- **Before:** a charter hauler pilot, signed on for one trip ("thousands of people asleep behind me") and meant to go back with the next lane ship. On landfall day she flew the landers down to Torvane when the first storm caught one of them; everyone aboard was lost (their names are on Nell's counter). She stayed.
- **Why here:** the haulers were grounded as the colony's freighters; Brannik needed a pilot who'd fly into weather.
- **Wants:** everyone out, alive. **Flaw:** nervous of the storms, snaps at people who are late ("please don't make me wait for you"). **Secret:** she still has her ticket home on a lane ship, never used.
- **Arc:** act 2 she tells the lander story (a talk with Fenna); act 4 the colony needs hauler pilots and she teaches them; act 6 she's offered the convoy's lead ship and turns it down to fly the crew's ship last.
- **Ties:** Wren looks up to her, Brannik trusts her, she carries the main story with Selin over the radio.
- **Voice:** quick and warm, a pilot's calm on the radio that cracks a little in the storm.

### Okoro (engineer, about fifty)

- **Before:** a drive engineer who studied stars on the side ("I came out here to study the star"), came with the colony to run Ostra's power. He applied to Calder's expedition and wasn't taken, which still stings.
- **Why here:** the only way to get close to Kesh; the heat shield is his design.
- **Wants:** to understand Kesh before it kills anyone. **Flaw:** proud, sure of his numbers. **Secret:** a notebook of every reading, with a forecast in it he believes.
- **Arc:** act 2 he finds the storm cycle; act 3 **his mistake**: his forecast says the next storms will be milder, the council uses it to calm the colony, and they're worse; people are hurt (Ilse counts them). Act 4 he owns it and works with Pell's data, and what the colony builds becomes his work; act 6 it holds.
- **Ties:** argues with Dace (the signature ores for guns or for the colony's way out), respects Hollis, resents Calder's memory a little until the logs win him over.
- **Voice:** precise, enthusiastic about the star, lectures when excited.

### Ilse (medic, mid-fifties)

- **Before:** a trauma doctor from home, the colony's chief doctor since landfall ("I used to run the clinic at Ostra"). She quit after the council kept her injury numbers out of its reports to the charter.
- **Why here:** she'd rather patch up the people who take the risks than read reports that lie; Brannik asked.
- **Wants:** the truth said out loud. **Flaw:** distrusts everyone in charge, Selin above all; blunt to the point of unkind. **Secret:** a copy of every report she wrote, and of every one the council sent.
- **Arc:** act 3 her numbers and Fenna's rumor crack the council's calm; act 4 she nurses Pell back from the cryo bay; act 5 she and Selin finally talk; act 6 she keeps the convoy's sick list.
- **Ties:** Selin (distrust, old respect underneath), Okoro (she was right in act 3, he was wrong), mothers Wren a little.
- **Voice:** calm, dry, exact; says the hard thing first.

### Dace (gunsmith, mid-thirties)

- **Before:** a machinist from the Torvane settlements, now at a stall at Ostra. The expedition hired him to make parts for its prototypes ("designs no colony workshop had ever made"), and he kept copies of the drawings.
- **Why here:** to build them for real; nobody at Ostra has the ores.
- **Wants:** to make something that matters. **Flaw:** more excited about the weapons than about why they're needed; careless with himself (the soot, the burns). **Secret:** some of his drawings aren't finished: the expedition was still working on them when it left.
- **Arc:** act 1 joins; act 3 finds out what the prototypes were made against (the hive's inner strains, from Calder's notes); act 5 he and Okoro stop arguing and build together; act 6 Zenith.
- **Ties:** Okoro (friction), Brannik (respect: Brannik tests everything he builds), Wren (the two young ones aboard).
- **Voice:** fast, chatty, technical, jokes.

### Wren (suit tech, early twenties)

- **Before:** born on Ostra, never off the station before the crew; grew up among the survey's station hands. Her father is one of the oldest of them, a docking tech. She learned suits fixing the station's EVA suits.
- **Why here:** she asked: her first chance to see a real planet.
- **Wants:** to see everything once. **Flaw:** reckless curiosity, takes things apart. **Secret:** she hasn't told her father she's flying further in with every act.
- **Arc:** act 1 joins; act 2 steps out onto a planet for the first time (at a safe landing, a small beat); act 5 her father is one of those who won't leave: she goes with the convoy, he stays in the shelter with Selin.
- **Ties:** looks up to Tamsin, Brannik protective of her, Dace her age.
- **Voice:** bright, curious, asks questions back.

### Hollis (miner mechanic, late sixties)

- **Before:** came with the survey 25 years ago as its drill operator and has prospected ever since ("longer than anyone else aboard"); guided Calder's expedition through the outer planets. Calder asked him to come inward; he said he was too old.
- **Why here:** found by the crew in Hollow Mire, without a crew and too stubborn to stop.
- **Wants:** to make it right. **Flaw:** gruff, won't talk about Calder until he has to. **Secret:** he's been hoping Calder is alive; Calder's last log is addressed to him.
- **Arc:** act 1 joins (and knows the ship); act 2 stories of the survey days (a talk with Brannik); act 4 at the base, he reads Calder's last log alone; act 6 at Calder's last site.
- **Ties:** teases Brannik, knew Calder and Rook from the old days, the only one Okoro listens to about the planets.
- **Voice:** slow, few words, old prospectors' sayings.

### Fenna (cook, mid-forties)

- **Before:** cooked in the settlements' canteen, then for Rook's crew for two years ("I signed on to cook, but mostly I listen"); left after Rook took his crew too close to the storms once too often.
- **Why here:** joins at the end of act 1: a crew that comes back is worth cooking for.
- **Wants:** a full table at the end. **Flaw:** gossip; can't keep a good story to herself. **Secret:** she knows why Rook's crew turned back from the inner planets a year ago.
- **Arc:** act 1 joins; act 3 **the rumor that turns out true**: an old station hand tells her the survey saw signs of the star and smoothed them over; act 5 her dish per planet; act 6 the last gathering.
- **Ties:** knows everyone's business, Tamsin's friend (the mess table), Rook's former cook.
- **Voice:** warm, teasing, stories with a point.

## Waypost Ostra

### Kade (depot master)

- **Before:** the charter's logistics clerk, sent with the colony to keep its account ("I keep the books"); never been down to a planet.
- **Wants:** the books to balance. **Flaw:** sees people as numbers until he meets them (why he wants to know the crews). **Secret:** his books showed a year ago that the colony couldn't feed itself without the crews; he told the charter, not the colony.
- **Arc:** his requests (the colony's real needs) all along; act 4 he plans the evacuation's lists, who goes on which hauler, the hardest list he's ever kept; act 6 he goes down to a planet for the first time, to an outpost being emptied.
- **Ties:** Selin (works for her, doesn't always agree), Nell (owes her).
- **Voice:** precise, numbers in every sentence, a shy warmth.

### Selin (council member)

- **Before:** the survey's station manager 25 years ago ("back when this station was little more than a docking ring"); when the colony came, the station hands chose her for the council.
- **Wants:** the colony to hold together. **Flaw:** careful with words to the point of hiding things ("a careless word travels fast"). **Secret:** she saw the old survey reading 25 years ago and believed the report's summary; she's afraid she should have known.
- **Arc:** act 1 hires the crew; act 3 doesn't want panic; act 4 decides the colony leaves and tells everyone; act 5 tells Ilse the truth; act 6 stays behind in Ostra's shelter with those who won't leave.
- **Ties:** Ilse (distrust), Kade, Wren's father (the old station hands).
- **Voice:** measured, formal, never a wasted word.

### Rook (rival captain)

- **Before:** about 20 years a prospector ("came in on one of the first haulers"); the biggest crew in the system.
- **Wants:** to be the best, and paid for it. **Flaw:** brag and bluster. **Secret:** a year ago his crew went inward after the expedition went silent, to be first at its base and its finds; a storm caught them, they turned back and lost a man. He never told anyone why they went.
- **Arc:** act 1 sneers; acts 2–4 his traces and the distress call (the players get his crew out); act 4 admits what happened; act 5 joins the effort; act 6 his ship flies with the convoy.
- **Ties:** Fenna (his former cook), Hollis (the old days), Nell (his tab).
- **Voice:** loud, mocking, a good laugh; quiet when it matters.

### Nell (bartender)

- **Before:** came with the colony as an air recycling tech, then took over the bar.
- **Wants:** her bar to be where people come back to. **Flaw:** keeps secrets as currency. **Secret:** one of the names on her counter she scratched in herself: someone on the lander Tamsin lost.
- **Arc:** her rumors per planet, the toast and the names on the counter all game; act 6 she unscrews the counter's top with the names and takes it onto the convoy.
- **Ties:** everyone; Tamsin (the lander), Rook (his tab), Kade (his debt).
- **Voice:** easy, knowing, a bartender's half-jokes.

## Still to come

Each gets a sheet in its act's writing round; none of them is met in act 1.

- **Pell** (act 4): the expedition's youngest, a technician, its one survivor, through Maren. Recovers at Ostra after the rescue (Ilse brings them back), talkable there; goes along to the base and the lane watch as a passenger for quest steps, on the radio during runs.
- **Calder and Maren:** dead; in the expedition's logs (voiced, each in their own voice) and in Pell's memories. Calder went to Zairen for Aethrine because he had found the beacon failing.
- **Ostra Control:** a voice on the radio only, one of the old station hands (maybe Wren's father on shift).
- **The beacon keeper** (act 3): runs the lane watch with two or three hands, the first to know the beacon is dying.
- **Wren's father** (Ostra, from act 2): a docking tech, one of the oldest station hands; won't leave in act 5.
- **Rook's first mate** (at the bar with Rook from act 2): the one whose friend Rook lost a year ago.
- **The smaller stations' people** (2–3 each), the outposts' quest givers, the colonists with threads (the teacher, the hauler pilot).

Everyone in this list except Calder, Maren and Ostra Control is a person to talk to like the twelve (a model, a voice, topics and quests), about two or three new people per act.
