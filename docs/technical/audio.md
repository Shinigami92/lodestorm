# Sound effects

- **Sound effects** (see [Sound](../design/sound.md)): synthesized by `assets/audio/source/build.py` (Python's standard library only, run with the system Python): `synth.py` is the kit (oscillators with anti-aliasing, noise, envelopes, biquad filters with sweeps, struck partials, echo, a small reverb, the finishing: DC removal, trimming the silent tail, short fades, the peak at -1 dBFS) and registers the recipes; a recipe is a function `(rng, take, variant) -> samples` in a module per group (`weapons.py`, `player.py`, `aliens.py`, `mining.py`, `mission.py` with the biomes' hazards, `refinery.py`, `stations.py`), decorated with `@sound("<group>/<name>", variants=)` (`loop=True` for a loop: the recipe makes `loop_seconds(s)`, the build crossfades its end into its start and writes a WAV sampler chunk looping it whole, which Godot's importer reads as the loop points; the listening page loops it) (a new sound can have `takes="abc"` to compare, the recipe branching on `take`; once one is picked, the others are removed). Every variant is seeded from its name, take and number, so a rebuild writes the same bytes. `python assets/audio/source/build.py [name ...]` writes the take the game uses as `assets/audio/sfx/<group>/<name>_<n>.wav` (16-bit mono, 44.1 kHz; Godot's importer compresses them with QOA, its default) and lists every sound with how it was made (`made_by`), its variants and the recordings it uses in `assets/audio/source/manifest.json`; `--takes <dir>` writes every take with `listen.html` (pick a take per sound, add notes, copy the summary) for picking by ear, e.g. into `tmp/sfx/takes/`. Recorded raw files go in `assets/audio/source/recordings/` with an entry in the manifest's `recordings` (source URL, author, license `CC0-1.0` or `CC-BY-4.0`) and are named in the recipe's `recordings`; the build refuses a recording that isn't listed with an allowed license, and `tests/project/test_sound_licenses.gd` checks every built file against the manifest. The source folder has a `.gdignore`. **Replacing a placeholder:** save the new sound over `assets/audio/sfx/<group>/<name>_<n>.wav` (16-bit WAV, mono for sounds in the world, any rate; keep the names; with more or fewer variants, also add or remove them in the `.tres`'s randomizer and change `variants`), set its manifest entry's `made_by` to how it was made instead of `"build.py"` (e.g. the AI model and its version, `"recorded"`, the tool; this is also the list for Steam's AI content disclosure) and list the recordings it uses (each with its `recordings` entry and raw file). The build then never overwrites it, and its recipe can be deleted. An AI model's license must allow selling the game (see `planned/sfx.md`). In the game, each sound is a `Sound` resource next to its files (`assets/audio/sfx/<group>/<name>.tres`: an `AudioStreamRandomizer` with the variants and the random pitch, the bus, `volume_db`, `limit`, `unit_size`, `max_distance`), referenced by the scenes and settings that play it (e.g. `WeaponType.attack_sound`); a new variant count means adding its streams there. The autoload `Sfx` (`game/audio/sfx.gd`) plays them: `play_at(sound, where, critical)` as an `AudioStreamPlayer3D` under itself (pausable), `play_ui(sound, bus)` flat (always, e.g. a volume slider's sample), `play_loop_at(sound, holder)` / `play_loop(sound, holder)` a loop under the node that makes it (following it, pausing with it, outside the limits) until `stop_loop` or the node goes; `play_at` takes a pitch (an alien strain's `voice_pitch`), which stretches its length; it counts each sound's voices down by its `length()` (the dummy audio driver of headless runs never finishes a stream), drops the farthest beyond the limit, ducks the Effects and Ambience buses while a critical cue plays and applies `GameSettings.volume` to the buses (`game/audio/bus_layout.tres`) every frame. Its `played(sound, critical)` signal is for tests. The listener is an `AudioListener3D` in `PlayerSounds`, made current for the local player; `PlayerSounds` also plays a step per `STEP_LENGTH` walked: a ground hazard's own `step_sound` while the player stands in it (shards, mud), else the deck's when a ray down hits something under a `Ship`, else the ground's. The other sounds come from where they happen: `AlienSounds` (each alien's child: chitter, strikes, hits, death; the attacks' `wind_up_sound` and `act_sound`), the nest, the miner (its loops), the ship (the ramp), the vent, and `MissionSounds` (the weather's child: the warning, the countdown, the storm, for the local viewer). **SceneTree scripts** (`-s`) compile before the autoloads exist, so a probe that refers to game classes (which refer to `Sfx`) fails to compile: load the probe's game code at runtime from a small loader script, or start the work after the first frame (`tools/translations.gd` awaits `process_frame` in `_initialize`).
