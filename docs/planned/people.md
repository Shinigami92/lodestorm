# People's own models (planned)

Decided 2026-10-24, built in parts with the art pass (M5, see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. The way people are made and Nell (the first) are built (see [`TECHNICAL.md`](../TECHNICAL.md#4-asset-pipeline), People, and Waypost Ostra in [`GAME_DESIGN.md`](../GAME_DESIGN.md)); what's left:

- **Everyone gets a MakeHuman body:** the other three at Waypost Ostra next (Kade, Selin, Rook), each a short build script on the shared pipeline with their sliders, their clothes and their own garments; then the crew after their own round of looks (age, gender and clothes per person, decided before their voices are designed); the colonists last, a few bodies with mixed genders and ages (the batch-8 concepts were all men) dressed per role, at about 10k triangles each.
- **Clothes, three kinds:** cloth that bends comes from MakeHuman's CC0 packs where one fits (retinted) or is built on the body like Nell's apron (an apron for the cook and the gunsmith too); hard parts (buckles, pouches, slates, plates, helmets) are rigid, each on one bone; hair from MakeHuman. Held props (Kade's slate, Selin's tablet, Rook's helmet under his arm) come with talk gestures, as accessories on a hand bone; until then they hang from the belt or are left out.
- **Selin's long coat:** below the hips weighted like Nell's apron (from the hips into both thighs), so it holds up if she ever walks.
- **Assets:** CC0 by default; a CC-BY asset only when nothing CC0 fits, recorded with its author in `assets/CREDITS.md`, which the store page and the game's credits read from.
- **Faces later:** a neutral face for now (no blinking, no mouth); MakeHuman's expression and mouth shapes (visemes) stay out of the export until lip sync and blinking get their own round, with the voice lines.
- **Fighting people:** every person has the same skeleton, so the player's weapon and melee clips can be baked for an NPC who fights (a mission situation), with the IK targets fitted to their body.
- **The player's suit:** the suit's hard parts (plates, helmet, backpack, canisters) split off the generated mesh or remade, each on one bone, only the cloth between them skinned; the visor its own glossy surface reflecting the scene instead of the reflection painted into its texture; later a MakeHuman body under the armor, so the player shares the skeleton with everyone.
- **Budget:** about 35k triangles for a named person (Nell: the body 22k, MakeHuman's proxy meshes aren't smaller), textures up to 2048 for the skin and 1024 for the rest; to look at again when many people are on screen at once.
