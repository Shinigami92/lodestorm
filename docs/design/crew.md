# Crew and NPCs

- **NPCs:** the crew (vendors at the stations, quest givers and story characters), each with a crew cabin. NPCs give quest chains; finishing their quests unlocks new items and upgrades from them, and their next quests.
  - **The crew:** eight, each at the post where they explain what happens there, each with a crew cabin (1–8). No captain: the players are the crew's prospectors and run the ship. Names are invented and untranslated, each with its own first letter; the German role word follows the character ("Pilotin", "Ingenieur"). Brannik, Tamsin, Okoro and Ilse are aboard from the start; Dace, Wren, Hollis and Fenna join in act 1 (see Crew joining below).

    | Name | Role | Where they stand (greybox) | Explains |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | **Brannik** | quartermaster | armory, in front of the weapon wall | ammo, spare magazines, reloading, the timing reload, the range |
    | **Tamsin** | pilot | bridge, between the holo table and the door from the lift lobby; in the cockpit's pilot seat while the ship flies | the navigator, picking a region, flying, Waypost Ostra |
    | **Okoro** | engineer | engine room, beside the drive core | the heat shield, what lies further in, the hold, the refinery |
    | **Ilse** | medic | medical bay, between the med beds | wounds carrying over, the med beds, falling out there |
    | **Dace** | gunsmith | weapons room, beside the counter | upgrades, upgrades taking time, building weapons from blueprints |
    | **Wren** | suit tech | armor room, beside the counter | the suit armor, the skills next door, the suit ring |
    | **Hollis** | miner mechanic | miners' room, beside the counter | placing miners, the fleet, drills, dry nodes |
    | **Fenna** | cook | mess hall, by a table | who's where aboard, news at Ostra |

    Each has a greeting, their post's topics and one about themselves (a hint of their personal thread, see [`planned/story.md`](../planned/story.md)); every topic opens once the greeting is heard, and none names locked content (the furnaces are cold, so the engineer says so; blueprints are needed, never where they come from). The stripped ship is said where it shows: Brannik's tour stop in the miners' bay ("They sold the cradles with everything else that came loose. We got two back."), Hollis' miners topic (a run takes as many miners as there are cradles; every cradle back means one more miner) and Okoro's refinery topic (the burners were sold before Brannik bought the ship). **Telling them apart:** each has their own model (a MakeHuman body in generated clothes with their generated head, see [`technical/characters.md`](../technical/characters.md)): shipboard work clothes of their own, worn and practical, no armored suit (only the players wear one) and no uniform; shared by everyone: a thin glowing collar band in their own color and a small round crew patch on the shoulder. Brannik, early sixties, broad, gray-white stubble, a dark olive padded vest with magazine pouches over a charcoal jumpsuit, black wristbands; Tamsin, late thirties, an auburn bun, a headset with a mic, a dark flight jacket and fingerless gloves; Okoro, about fifty, tall, shaved head, welding goggles pushed up, an off-white work jacket under rust-brown bib overalls, leather work gloves; Ilse, mid-fifties, ash-blond hair tied back, thin glasses, a long pale medical coat with a cross on the chest, a harness with medical pouches, black gloves; Dace, mid-thirties, wiry, goggles pushed up, a scorched leather apron over a work shirt with rolled sleeves, sooty forearms; Wren, early twenties, a black bob with teal tips, a light-grey technician jumpsuit with pushed-up sleeves, fingerless gloves; Hollis, late sixties, a white stubble beard, a hard hat with a lamp, an olive canvas jacket; Fenna, mid-forties, a Black woman with curly hair under a cap, a cook's jacket with rolled sleeves, a long dark apron with a towel. Four women and four men, as the German role words have it.
  - **The crew aboard** (greybox, values **TBD (playtest)**): each crew member stands at their spot (the quartermaster in the armory, in front of the weapon wall, so it never hides him; the pilot between the holo table and the bridge's door from the lift lobby, so a player coming in doesn't walk around the table to her) and turns towards a player who comes close (3 m). **They walk, they never appear somewhere:** a crew member on their way finds it over the ship's floors around walls, stations and furniture, the doors opening for them like for players, looks where they go, stops while a player is within 3 m and goes on once they've left; while someone talks to them they stay put. Once a destination is chosen ("Set as destination" at the navigator or on the quest board), or when the ship flies without one (the take-off after a mission), Tamsin walks to the cockpit at a brisker pace (2 m/s instead of 1.3, stopping for nobody; about 20 s from the bridge, so on a short take-off she arrives as it ends), sits down in the pilot seat (0.45 m, with a backrest, facing the flight console and the windows), sets course herself 1.5 s after sitting down once the destination's location is scanned, everyone is aboard and nobody of the crew still walks aboard (a player at the flight console can set it sooner; in co-op the host's game decides) and watches the autopilot; if the destination goes away without a flight, she walks back; once the ship has landed or docked (or she has arrived after a short flight), she stays seated 5 s, gets up and walks back to the bridge. Players can talk to her while she sits (her bubble lower). Between the decks they take the lift by the lobby like a player: they walk to its gate, call the platform, step on (it waits for them, also with players already on it), ride along and step off on the other deck. **They don't just stand there:** now and then (3–8 s of standing between, never the same twice in a row, never while a player is within 3 m) everyone looks around over their shoulders, stretches with their hands behind the head or checks something on their wrist, and each walks between their own spot and two work spots in their room every 20–40 s, doing there what fits the place: tapping at a console (Tamsin at the holo table, Brannik at the lockers, Okoro at the drive core, Wren at the fitting frame, Hollis at the miner on its pad, Fenna at the mess hall's table) or looking around and checking their wrist (Tamsin at the bridge's window, Brannik in front of the weapon wall, Okoro watching the core, Ilse at two of the med beds, Dace at his bench and at the rack, Wren at the counter, Hollis at the cradles, Fenna at the window). Their own spot is where players find them first. **They know where they are:** the room they stand in (a doorway still counts as the room they come from), and their own room, the one of their spot (Brannik's armory, Tamsin's bridge). Outside it they say what fits anywhere: a line that points at their room ("Welcome to the weapons room", "come and look at this") has a second line for elsewhere in the same topic, the place named instead (Dace: "The weapons room is mine"; Tamsin in the cockpit: "every trip starts at the navigator on the bridge"); where one line fits in their room and elsewhere alike, it's the only one ("one of my med beds", "right next to my armory"). No line names a deck or a direction to it ("come up", "on the lower deck"): the player may stand on either. What they say while the list shows fits anywhere. Work with things in their hands (Dace grinding, Fenna cooking) comes with each one's own round (see [`planned/people.md`](../planned/people.md)).
  - **Brannik's tour** (values **TBD (playtest)**): on a new game Brannik waits in the corridor in front of the player cabins (an amber marker above him), and his first talk there offers to show the ship ("Before the first mission, let me walk you through the ship once...", without treating the player as new aboard; his armory greeting comes later, at the armory). The card then offers "Show me the ship" and "Goodbye"; leaving it there sends him to the armory, and "Show me the ship" stays among his topics, so the tour can be asked for any time. Players who leave him waiting and choose their first destination send him to the armory too (the offer unheard: his armory greeting comes first, and "Show me the ship" is still among his topics). Heard to the end, the talk closes and the tour starts: he walks ahead at 2 m/s (stopping for nobody, he leads) through twelve stops: the mess hall, the bridge, the cockpit, the lift (which he takes down), the medical bay, the engine room, the shooting range, the weapons room, the armor room, the skill graph, the miners' bay and his armory, where it ends ("The cargo bay and the ramp are right through there"). At each stop he waits until the player is within 4 m, then says its line in the bubble above his head (his name plate hidden meanwhile) and in his voice, each as long as a line moving on by itself, and walks on: about four minutes for the whole ship. Talking to him during the tour offers "Go on" and "End the tour" only (ending it sends him to the armory; the topic stays); a talk in the middle of a stop starts its line over afterwards. If the player stays farther than 25 m for 30 s (or falls), he ends it by himself. In co-op, whoever asked leads it; the others may tag along. What's heard is saved as usual (the offer once heard, a new game's wait is over); a tour itself isn't saved, a save loads with him at the armory. A name plate with their name and role ("Brannik · Quartermaster") shows while a player is within 4 m, and an amber marker floats above their head while they have something nobody has heard yet (a diamond while they offer a quest or one of its steps can be handed in at them, see [Quests](missions.md#quests)). They play their model's idle and walk, lit by a small work light.
  - **Transmissions** (values **TBD (playtest)**): the crew notices what happens and calls in over the radio when it happens, one crew member per event. Beside the player (to the left of their head on screen) shows the dark bubble without a tail, its top row an antenna and the caller ("Tamsin · Radio") in their collar color, the line below, as wide as the line needs and grown with the view; it stays as long as a line moving on by itself (its voice and 1 s, else 2 s + 0.06 s per letter) and never blocks. Calls coming in meanwhile wait their turn (1 s apart); a newer call about the same thing replaces one still waiting. Their voice plays on the Radio bus (see Sound). Calls go to every player, or only to the player they're about; when several players are together, the lines heard by everyone are worded for the group (e.g. German "kommt" instead of "komm"). Lines never depend on where the player stands, since a call can come anywhere.

    | Event | When | Who calls | Lines |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | The first run won (once per game) | everyone alive back aboard | Brannik | one, alone or together |
    | The first fall (once per game) | waking up in the medical bay, only to the one who fell | Ilse | one |
    | A new planet opened by a heat shield plate | the plate installed | the one with a line for that planet: Okoro (Vehra, Skelde), Tamsin (Cassor, Zairen), Fenna (Gaulm's moons) | one per planet |
    | A new weapon built | right after building, only to the one who built it | Dace and Brannik taking turns (those aboard) | three each, in turn |
    | A big haul: a run's whole haul (all players' together) beating the biggest so far by a quarter, from the second won run on | everyone back aboard | Okoro, Hollis and Brannik taking turns (those aboard) | three each, in turn |
    | The first landing on a planet or moon (once per body) | the first player stepping out | Tamsin | one per body |
    | The storm's warning | when it starts (with the horn), only to the players outside | Tamsin | four in turn, alone or together |
    | The late deposit | the meteor's impact | Tamsin | three in turn, alone or together |

    During a run the radio carries only what matters out there: the storm warning, the first landing, the late deposit and later quest steps and story moments, mostly from Tamsin in the cockpit. The turns and the biggest haul are the host's, saved with the ship. Listening to calls again aboard (the comms log) comes with the quests; planned: other crew members' calls sounding like they come from the cockpit beside the pilot (see [`planned/voice.md`](../planned/voice.md)).
  - **Crew joining** (16 C3a–C3c, values **TBD (playtest)**; the other three join quests: see [`planned/crew.md`](../planned/crew.md)): a crew member who joins later names the story flag they join with (`NpcProfile.join_flag`, `crew.<id>.joined`), set by a quest step's hand-in (`QuestStep.joins`, the card's reward "Joins the crew: Dace"); Dace, Wren, Hollis and Fenna have one, the other four none. Until it's set they aren't at their post (hidden, still, nobody can talk to them), don't call in over the radio (the turns only take those aboard) and aren't in overheard talks; their stations work without them. **Stand-in topics** (`Topic.until`, gone once the flag is set) cover their posts: Brannik explains the weapons room (Dace's), Ilse the suit's armor and skills (Wren's), Okoro the miners (Hollis'); the cook has no station. **Lines name someone only once they're aboard:** a topic or a transmission naming them requires their flag (Fenna's crew list, Brannik's big haul line about Dace and Wren: a reaction with `CrewReaction.requires` goes before the one without and repeats its lines), and Brannik's tour stops at their rooms say a line for nobody there yet (`TourStop.empty_lines`: "We still need someone for this room. Until then, the bench works on its own."); a test checks every crew line and overheard talk naming one of them. **Heat shield MK2 (Vehra) waits for the whole crew** (`ShieldPlate.needs_whole_crew`): the card says "The whole crew has to be aboard first." and can't make it the project; the only gate besides the heat shield, later plates need nothing more. The debug overlay's "Join the crew" brings everyone aboard ("Install shield" and "Open all" too); the tests' map fixture starts with everyone joined, `instantiate(false)` with a new game's crew. **Waiting at Ostra and walking aboard** (16 C3b): until they join, Dace, Wren and Fenna wait at their places at Waypost Ostra (a second NPC of their profile there, `Npc.waits_to_join`): Dace at his stall in the concourse (his workbench behind the counter, the colonist mechanic at the next stall), Wren at her suit workbench in the hangar by the side door to the depot, Fenna sitting on the back bench of Rook's booth in the bar (`Npc.seated`: an NPC sitting at their own spot, getting up to go). Nobody talks to them there yet and they carry no marker (their topics before joining come with the join quests). Once their flag is set while the ship is docked there, they get up and walk aboard (`NpcBoarding`, at walking pace, stopping for nobody): over the station's navigation to the foot of the ramp, waiting there while it isn't down, up it and a step in through the door, where their crew member aboard takes over (`Npc.arrive`) and walks on to their post, stopping for nobody either. Meanwhile the ship doesn't leave (the flight console says "Wait until Dace is aboard.") and the ramp button can't raise the ramp (it can still lower it). With the ship elsewhere (or a save loaded after they joined) they're at their post at once. Their places stay empty afterwards: Dace's stall with its shutter down (`StoryShown`, scenery following a story flag) and his sign still up, Wren's bench with her tools left on it, Rook's booth one person less. Until the join quests exist (C3d), a new game still starts with the whole crew aboard (`Ship.crew_joins_later` off), so the places stay empty; switching it on in the ship's scene and joining with the debug overlay's "Join the crew" while docked at Ostra shows the walk. **Hollis out there** (16 C3c), the first person in a generated location and the way later ones work (see Someone out there in [Missions](missions.md#quests)): Kade's "Overdue" sends the players to Hollow Mire, where Hollis waits at his camp (a map part at a middle distance from the ship) while a step of it is active, beside his broken old miner. An old survey repeller post at his camp keeps aliens off (`AlienRepeller`: none come within 12 m, and nobody within its reach is their prey, players and miners included), and he carries a hand repeller (4 m) on his way to the ship; no nest grows within 30 m of his camp. He's talked to there (his quest's entry and "About Hollis", a topic that goes once he joins, which also explains the repeller; no greeting; while the list shows he says the open step's prompts, `QuestStep.place_prompts`: "Finally, somebody found me. I could use a hand with one of my old miners.", later "Any luck with that coupling?"; without one, prompts of his own there, `Dialogue.place_prompts`, never the crew's "the miners won't fix themselves"); solo the run stops while the talk is open (aliens, the timer, the storm), in co-op it goes on and the others cover whoever talks. Both steps are handed in at him during the run: the first once his camp is found, the second once the nest at his dig site (a map part far out in the region, on the vein he was working) is down and a player brings the drive coupling (from a crate of the region). Both are there from the start. Then he joins: once the talk ends, he packs up his miner (it shrinks away), walks to the ship along the passes at 2.2 m/s, waits at the ramp's foot while it's up, goes up it and his crew member aboard walks on to the miners' room; the ship doesn't leave meanwhile ("Wait until Hollis is aboard."), and the run doesn't end with the players aboard: the ramp stays down until he's through the door. From a camp in the middle of the region the walk takes one to two minutes. A lost run takes it back: he stops where he is, and the next visit finds him at his camp with the step that was open before. From then on his old miner stands in the cargo bay by the ramp (a prop, not an extra miner: miners come from the cradles), and Kade has a topic about him.
  - **Talking to them:** players start it themselves (`interact`), like using a station: the player stays where they stand, the camera frames both, and one player talks to them at a time. Their line shows in a speech bubble above their head (the whole line at once) and is spoken in their voice where it's recorded (see [Localization and voice](localization-and-voice.md)), and a card beside them lists the topics they can talk about and "Goodbye" while their bubble asks what the player wants (one of four prompts in their own words, picked at random for each talk, never the one of the talk before, e.g. Brannik's "Something on your mind?") (past seven entries the list scrolls, keeping the selected one in view); topics nobody has heard yet carry an amber dot. While a player uses a menu or talks, the name plates of others close by hide (the camera moves in close). A topic plays a few lines (`interact` or "Continue" moves on), then the list comes back; `pause`/B or "Back" steps back to the list, and closes from there. The first talk starts with their greeting. Topics open up as the story goes on (e.g. the timing reload once reloading was heard), a topic about a weapon only once the player owns it (no talk about locked weapons), a topic about a planet or moon only once the ship reaches it, and every topic can be heard again any time, so what the crew explains can always be looked up again. Quests add their own entries at the top of the list, set apart by an amber diamond and a line below them (see [Quests](missions.md#quests)). There's no branching story in a conversation: players ask and accept. Lines are full, plain sentences (up to about five rows in the bubble), not clipped ones, so they stay easy to follow in every language. **NPCs only know their world:** they talk about the storm, the ramp, the holo table, the consoles, the lights on the gear and the silos, never about what only exists for the player: the timer and its countdown, the ring around the player's feet, the holo cards and their buttons, the settings, saving, topics or markers; where a card shows something, the NPC says it themselves ("I'll tell you what it costs", "set it as our destination"). The game knows whether the player talking is the host or a guest in co-op, so a topic can tell a guest something else (Okoro's heat shield: the host decides on it, a guest helps pay through the runs). What's been heard is saved with the ship (the host's).
  - **The quartermaster** (Brannik) runs the armory and explains the ammo: rounds made from carried ore, the spare magazines (from the ship's stock, not rebuilt during a run, the cells on the backpack), reloading (Harl throws away the rest of its magazine, slower walking), the timing reload, Brask once the player owns it (shell by shell, timed shells in a row) and the shooting range next door (try new weapons there before a mission, free reloads to practice).
