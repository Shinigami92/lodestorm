# Aliens

- **Enemies:** only aliens for now, one species in several strains and roles: eleven hunting strains, a guard, a raider and the apex, the Ravager (more guards and raiders may come, see [`planned/aliens.md`](../planned/aliens.md)). A **strain** is the body (how it moves, what it takes, its attacks), a **role** what it goes for: **guards** stay by their nest, **hunters** go for players, **raiders** for miners. Every strain plays one role, so the body tells the role: the warden guards, the gnawer raids, the swarmer, the mite, the spitter, the burrower, the brute, the shrieker, the bloater, the tender, the darter, the stalker and the skimmer hunt. A group of aliens that hatches together is a **pack** (hunters only). The Ravager isn't hatched by nests: it roams some locations on its own.

## Strains

| Tier | Strain | Size | How it fights | Weak to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Swarmer** | medium | a runner with a short pounce when close | anything |
| 1 | **Mite** | tiny | packs of 6–10, a quick bite; one hit kills | wide hits (Dray, Brask, Scald, Fulgen) |
| 2 | **Spitter** | medium | keeps away and lobs acid that leaves a burning puddle; flees when approached | range, the shield, charging at it |
| 2 | **Burrower** | medium | travels under the ground, bursts up under its target with a bite | moving; hitting it while it's up |
| 3 | **Brute** | big | armored front plates, a soft back; a telegraphed charge in a line | flanking, piercing (Quill, Auger), heavy hits |
| 3 | **Shrieker** | medium | stays behind the pack and screams: aliens nearby speed up and more come | focus fire, range |
| 4 | **Bloater** | big, slow | bursts next to a player into an acid cloud that hurts aliens too | killing it at range, near other aliens |
| 4 | **Tender** | medium | heals and hardens aliens near it with a pulsing link; stays back | focus fire |
| 5 | **Darter** | small, flying | packs flying low over cliffs and rock, diving at players | Fulgen, Brask, the target lock |
| 5 | **Stalker** | tall | see-through until close; its first strike from hiding hits hard, then hooks that slow; hit and run | Fulgen, wide hits, sticking together |
| 6 | **Skimmer** | medium, flying | circles out of reach of flat shots, spitting acid bolts on the move | the target lock, range |
| any | **Warden** (guard) | big | stays by its nest; front armor, a slam | flanking |
| any | **Gnawer** (raider) | medium | goes for working miners | defending the miners |
| 3+ | **Ravager** (apex) | huge | roams some locations, slams, charges, sheds swarmers | everything together; avoiding it is fine |

- The hive escalates over a run (see the nests below), and aliens are stronger on bodies farther in (see Tiers in [Missions](missions.md)); working miners draw raiders, and the end of a run brings the hive's frenzy (see the nests below).
- **Swarmer** (`game/aliens/strains/swarmer/`: the strain, its bite and its pounce; all values **TBD (playtest)**): see [Art direction](art.md#art-direction); its glowing eyes show where it faces. It waits until a living player outside the ship comes within 18 m, then chases the nearest one at 5.2 m/s (still slower than the player's 6, so running away works but isn't free) and bites once per second for 10 damage when in reach. A player gets away beyond 20 m or by going aboard: aliens don't hunt inside the ship. 30 HP; when it dies it collapses, lies still for a moment and sinks into the ground. Harl deals 10 damage per shot, so three hits kill one. Shots don't hurt other players (no friendly fire).
- **The pounce:** a swarmer 2.5–5 m from a player crouches for 0.4 s (the body drops, the eyes flare: the telegraph), then leaps up to 4 m at the spot where the player stood when the crouch ended, so a sidestep or a dash dodges it; if it comes within reach on the way it hits for 15. After landing it stands still for 0.8 s, an opening to hit back. It pounces at most every 4 s, and only at players (raiders bite miners).
- **Mite** (`game/aliens/strains/mite/`, tier 1): tiny, in packs of 6–10 (see the nests below), 10 HP, so any hit kills it; it scuttles at 5.6 m/s and bites for 3 every 0.8 s. Wide hits (Dray, Brask) clear a pack. It counts a quarter towards the cap on living aliens.
- **Spitter** (`game/aliens/strains/spitter/`, tier 2): 40 HP, slow (4.6 m/s), keeps away: it comes no closer than 14 m and backs off when a player gets within 9 m (cornered, it stays and spits). From 4 to 14 m it rears up for 0.6 s (the telegraph) and lobs a glowing glob of acid in an arc at where its target stands, over rocks and gaps; a ring on the ground closes in on the landing spot while it flies, so a step aside dodges it. It splashes 8 on whoever is within 1.2 m and leaves a burning puddle (1.5 m, 4 s, 6 HP/s) that hurts players and miners, not aliens (the hive's own acid). It spits every 3.5 s. Charging at it works.
- **Burrower** (`game/aliens/strains/burrower/`, tier 2): 45 HP, pushed less (70 %). For a target farther than 3 m it digs in and travels under the ground along the same paths as the others (gaps and walls still stop it): shots, swings and the target lock can't reach it, only a mound of earth throwing up clods shows where it is. Under its target the mound swells for 0.7 s (the telegraph), then it bursts up and bites whoever is still on it for 18; moving away in time dodges it. Then it stays up for 3 s and bites (10 every 1.2 s), and can be hit, before it digs in again for a target that got away. Without a target it comes up.
- **Warden** (`game/aliens/strains/warden/`, the guard): big and slow (3.6 m/s), 150 HP, armored at the front (6 off every hit within 140° of its front, so flanking pays), hard to stagger and barely pushed. It goes for players within 14 m but stays within 25 m of where it hatched: a player who runs beyond that gets away, and it walks back to its nest. Its slam: within about 2 m it rears up for 0.8 s (the telegraph), then brings its clubs down 1.4 m ahead, hitting every player within 1.6 m of that spot for 25; stepping out of it in time dodges it, and it stands still for a second afterwards.
- **Gnawer** (`game/aliens/strains/gnawer/`, the raider): fast (5.6 m/s), 35 HP, crushing jaws (14 every 0.8 s). It goes for the nearest working miner however far away and ignores players, biting only one who stands right in its way. Without a working miner it walks back to its nest and guards it like a warden (players within 12 m, within 20 m of where it hatched).
- **Brute** (`game/aliens/strains/brute/`, tier 3): a big rhino-beetle, 200 HP, slow to turn (so flanking works). Its layered plates cover the front (8 off every hit within 160°, and they take only 30 % of a hit's stagger), its back is soft (sinew with glowing slits). Its **charge**: a player 5–14 m away makes it lower its head for 1 s while its lane shows on the ground (red dashed edges, the fill growing towards the end of the lane as the charge comes), aimed where the player stood; then it runs along it at 13 m/s up to 3 m past that spot, hitting everyone in the lane once for 30. Stepping out of the lane dodges it. It runs through players; running into rock, a miner or another alien leaves it dazed for 1.5 s, its back open. Close up it gores (18). It charges at most every 6 s.
- **Shrieker** (`game/aliens/strains/shrieker/`, tier 3): a lean runner with a long neck and a glowing throat sac, 45 HP, easy to stagger. It stays back (11 m from its target, backing off when a player comes closer) and screams every 8 s: its neck rises and the sac swells for 1 s (the telegraph), then every alien within 15 m, itself too, is 25 % faster for 6 s and the nest nearest to it sends a pack, boosted the same (see the nests below). A ring sweeps over the ground to show the scream's reach. A few quick hits during the wind-up stagger it and stop the scream.
- **Bloater** (`game/aliens/strains/bloater/`, tier 4): a fat grub whose back is a swollen, veined bladder, on short stubby legs, 70 HP, slow (3.2 m/s), so it falls behind its pack. It goes for players, never for miners; next to its target its bladder swells and shudders for 0.5 s, then it bursts. It also bursts when it's killed, without a warning. The burst splashes 15 within 3 m and leaves an acid cloud there for 4 s (8 HP/s) that hurts players, miners and aliens alike, so shooting it from afar, ideally inside its pack, is the answer.
- **Tender** (`game/aliens/strains/tender/`, tier 4): a low six-legged alien with glowing gland clusters and three tendrils, 55 HP. It stays back (9 m) and links up to 3 aliens within 8 m with pulsing strands from its tendrils' tips, the most hurt first: each heals 5 HP/s and has 3 more armor on every side. A link breaks beyond 10 m or when either dies or goes under the ground; tenders don't link each other, and an alien takes one link at most. While it's staggered the strands dim and don't heal, so focus fire on it pays. Cornered, it lashes (8).
- **Darter** (`game/aliens/strains/darter/`, tier 5): a small flier with two pairs of see-through wings and a glowing sting, 20 HP, in packs (it counts half towards the cap). It flies 3 m above the ground, straight at its target over rock and cliffs (no paths), never sinking below its target's ground, so it crosses gaps level. Up there flat shots fly under it: only shots locked on it reach it (they fly at the alien), and swings don't. It keeps 4 m away; from 3.5 to 9 m it hovers for 0.6 s with its sting glowing while its line shows on the ground like the brute's lane, then dives along it at 14 m/s down to 1 m, stinging everyone in it once for 10. It stays low for 1 s, where every weapon reaches it, then climbs again. It dives at most every 3.5 s; killed, it drops to the ground.
- **Stalker** (`game/aliens/strains/stalker/`, tier 5): a tall, hunched ambush hunter on two legs with two huge scything talons over its shoulders, two smaller grasping claws, feeding tendrils around its mouth and dark mottled chitin, 70 HP. It hides: while no player is within 8 m it's see-through (only its silhouette behind cliffs shows) and the target lock can't take it, though shots and swings still hit it; within 8 m it's a shimmer bending what's behind it, its eyes a faint glint, and it can be locked on. When it starts an attack or takes a hit it shows fully for 3 s. From hiding it strikes: its first strike of an attack started while hidden hits for at least 30 (a pounce from 2.5–6 m with a 0.35 s wind-up, normally 22, or a talon slash close up, normally 12). Once it's been seen it also shoots flesh hooks at players 6–10 m away (0.5 s wind-up): a straight barbed hook on a sinew line that rock stops and a step aside dodges; it hits for 4 and holds the player to half their walking speed for 1.5 s (the dash still gets away; it never pulls, which would turn the view in VR). After each attack it backs off for 2 s and fades again: hit and run. Sticking together and wide hits help.
- **Skimmer** (`game/aliens/strains/skimmer/`, tier 6): a winged ray with a long whip tail and a glowing throat sac, 45 HP, in packs of 2–4. It flies 5 m up (only locked shots reach it) and never hovers: it keeps 10 m away and circles its target out to 14 m, spitting a fast, straight acid bolt every 2 s on the move (a ring on the ground shows where it lands) that splashes 8 within 1 m and leaves a small burning puddle (1 m, 3 s, 5 HP/s). Killed, it drops to the ground.
- **Ravager** (`game/aliens/strains/ravager/`, the apex): a huge armored monster on six pillar legs with a crest of horns and brood pods on its back, 1000 HP (scaled by tier like the rest), 3 armor everywhere, almost impossible to stagger or push. From tier 3 a location may have one (25 % on tier 3, 10 % more per tier up to 55 % on tier 6, picked by the location's seed), roaming between the areas away from the landing site. Its steps shake the view of players within 40 m, the more the closer, and throw up dust, so it's felt before it's seen. It goes for players within 22 m and lets them go beyond 30 m, slower than they are (3.6 m/s): avoiding it is fine. It sheds three swarmers from its pods when a player is within 20 m (every 20 s, only under the cap), charges along a lane like the brute's (7–18 m, 45, a 2.2 m wide lane, dazed for 2.5 s when it runs into something) and slams close up (it rears up for 1 s, then 40 within 3 m of the spot, shaking the ground hard). It drops nothing yet (the quests' hunt goal will reward it).
- **Buffed aliens show it:** while a scream boosts an alien or a tender's link holds it, it's 15 % bigger and outlined in the hive's yellow-green, so a buffed pack stands out from afar.
- **Telegraphs:** every heavy attack has a wind-up readable from the isometric camera (the body crouches or rears up, the eyes and glands brighten, 0.4–0.8 s) and a sound of its own (see [Sound](sound.md)). No hit-stop (co-op and VR don't take it well).
- **Hits:** each hit (a projectile, a swing) flashes the alien white, throws glowing ichor in the hive's yellow-green from its flesh or grey sparks and chips off armor (readable without numbers), pushes it back a little (Harl 0.5, a Brask pellet 0.8, Dray 6 m/s, fading within a fraction of a second; heavy strains less) and jolts its body.
  - **Armor:** a flat reduction per hit, but at least 25 % of the damage always gets through, so no alien is immune; many small hits suffer, heavy hits don't. A strain can be armored only on its front (the warden, the brute). A tender's link adds armor on every side. Burning ground (lava, vents) ignores armor. The swarmer has none.
  - **Stagger:** each hit fills a stagger meter by weapon (Harl 7, a Brask pellet 4, Dray 40); past the strain's threshold (swarmer 25) it's staggered for 0.5 s: it rears up, stops and its attack is broken off (also a pounce's wind-up). A stagger empties the meter, hits while staggered don't fill it, and the meter drains within a second. So a swarmer staggers after about six quick Harl hits, one close Brask blast or one Dray swing.
- **Limit:** at most 60 aliens live at once (40 in VR), small strains counting as a share (a mite a quarter, a darter half; the Ravager doesn't count); nests wait while it's reached. Guards take at most half of it, so on big maps with many nests the hunters and raiders still come (nests whose guards don't fit stand unguarded). The debug overlay's hatch shortcuts ignore it.
- **Pathfinding:** aliens walk around rocks and nests along a navigation mesh that the outside world bakes when it appears (miners placed later aren't part of it, aliens steer around them instead), and steer around each other, so swarms spread around their target instead of stacking.

## Nests and the hive

- **Greybox nests** (`game/aliens/nest/alien_nest.tscn`, values in `alien_nest_settings.tres`, all **TBD (playtest)**): dark crimson mounds on a slimy patch with a glowing mouth. When the outside world appears, a warden hatches beside each nest and guards it. Once the mission starts, each nest also sends out **hunters** in packs: aliens that go for the nearest player outside however far away and don't give up because of distance (only by the player going aboard or dying). The hive escalates with the mission's progress, from the start until the storm hits: a nest hatches a pack every 60 s at first and every 20 s at the end, while fewer of its hunters are out than 1 at first and 5 at the end (counted by their share, so one pack may go over it); once they're down below that, the next pack comes a full interval later. A pack hatches one alien after another, a quarter of a second apart, and aliens waiting to hatch count towards the cap. The glow in the mouth pulses faster as the hive escalates.
- **The frenzy:** from the storm's warning on (and during the storm), the hive frenzies: its aliens are 15 % faster and their eyes glow brighter, nests send hunters at their end values (a pack every 20 s, up to 5 out) and their mouths pulse at the fastest rate. It pushes the way back without new rules.
- **Destroying a nest** (values in `alien_nest_settings.tres`, all **TBD (playtest)**): every weapon hurts a nest, ranged and melee (a swing reaches it at its edge), so a melee-only loadout clears nests too. It has 600 health on a tier 1 body (more farther in, like the aliens, and +50 % per player beyond the first) and armor like the aliens' (1 off every hit, at least a quarter gets through), so heavy hits are better, never required. A hit calls its guards: they go for the attacker from any distance while that player is within their leash. **It fights back:** at two thirds and one third of its health its mouth swells and glows white for 1 s, then it bursts, spewing 3 hunters of its packs' strains one after another (under the cap); a hit taking it past both at once still brings both bursts, one after the other. **Its state, without a health bar:** after each burst glowing cracks open in its plates and its glow flickers lower. **Destroyed**, it collapses inwards, the glow goes dark and the slime dries grey over a few seconds; the dark husk stays as a landmark for the run, but hatches nothing more: its hunters and raiders stop coming (the aliens it already hatched stay), and every other nest sends its next pack right away, a short surge. With every nest gone, nothing hatches any more, only what's still out there; the storm still ends the run. Nothing drops: it pays off in safety, fewer hunters and raiders, and later quest progress (the "clear nests" goal). Every visit generates a new section, so the nests are back. The damage readout shows numbers on it like on aliens. Nests stay something to find: the location hologram doesn't show them and they have no silhouette behind cliffs, alive or destroyed.
- **A scream's answer:** when a shrieker screams, the nest nearest to it sends its next pack right away, boosted like the shrieker's (at most every 15 s per nest, and only under the cap); its mouth flares and a ring sweeps out from it, so the answer can be seen.
- **Packs and what hatches where** (`game/aliens/hive/hive.tres`, all **TBD (playtest)**): hunters come in packs from a list of recipes, e.g. two swarmers, eight mites, a swarmer with six mites, two swarmers and a spitter, a burrower with six mites, a swarmer, a burrower and a spitter; each nest picks one by weight among those whose strains all hatch there. Which strains hatch depends on the body's tier: the first planet's from the start, older tiers' from the start, the body's own new strains from halfway through a run (so they appear late in a run on the first planet that brings them, from the start on the next one), and hard regions add one strain of the next tier from three quarters of the run on (the location keeps the same one), so the next planet isn't a wall. Each biome favors some strains (burrowers in the Dust flats and Salt pans, darters in the Ash fields and Glass plains, skimmers in the Glass plains, stalkers in the Overgrowth): packs and raiders with them come twice as often, never only them, so no biome needs a particular weapon. Tier 5 brings packs of five darters, three darters with two swarmers, two stalkers, a stalker with six mites, four darters with a shrieker, a stalker with a tender and two swarmers, a brute with four darters, and two stalkers with a bloater; tier 6 brings the skimmer (two, three or four of them, two with three darters, one with a brute and two swarmers, two with a stalker) besides all the others mixed, and the Ravager more often.
- **Raiders:** nests also send out raiders (gnawers), one by one. A raider goes for the nearest working miner however far away, attacking it until it breaks down and then moving on to the next one. Once no miner works, it goes back and guards its nest. They only hatch while at least one miner works, the first one a full interval after that, and escalate like hunters with their own values: one every 60 s → 25 s, at most 1 → 2 per nest at once (**TBD (playtest)**). Defending miners means shooting raiders before they get there, or repairing behind them. **Miners draw them:** each working miner makes its nearest nest send raiders 10 % more often (20 % on a signature ore), so a big mining site pays off but gets busy.

## Alien look and behavior (direction for the art pass)

- **A hive of bio-organic beasts**, not soldiers or machines: insect- and reptile-like creatures with chitin plates over sinewy flesh, claws, mandibles and teeth, several legs, no tech, no clothes. They are one species in many strains, all sharing the same body language and colors, so any alien reads as "one of them" at a glance.
- **Swarms:** most aliens are small and come in numbers; they scuttle low over the ground, fast and erratic. Danger comes from many at once more than from a single one.
- **Strains:** the roster is in [Aliens](#strains). Looks from the concept rounds:
  - **Swarmer:** a lean, dog-sized runner on four clawed legs with a long toothed jaw and scythe-like bone blades on its back. Modeled close to the concept (see [Art direction](art.md#art-direction)).
  - **Brute:** a four-legged rhino-beetle with heavy layered armor plates and horns. The plates are chitin and must not read as riveted metal. Modeled: four layered plates over the front ending in a ragged edge, a long upward nose horn under a forward thoracic horn, a sinewy back with four glowing slits.
  - **Shrieker, bloater, tender:** modeled too: the shrieker a lean four-legged runner with a long ridged neck and a glowing throat sac that swells as it screams; the bloater a fat grub on short stubby legs (nothing spider-like, for players with arachnophobia) whose back is a veined bladder with glowing veins, popping to a husk when it bursts; the tender a low six-legged body with gland clusters on its back and three tendrils ending in glowing bulbs.
  - **Darter, stalker, skimmer, Ravager:** modeled too: the darter a slender wasp-like flier with two pairs of see-through wings, glowing bands across its abdomen and a glowing sting; the stalker a tall, hunched biped on digitigrade legs with two huge scything talons raised over its shoulders, smaller grasping claws, feeding tendrils around its mouth and darker mottled chitin with thin glowing seams; the skimmer a broad, plated ray with thick fleshy wings, a row of glowing spots along each wing's trailing edge, a long ringed whip tail with a glowing barb and a big glowing throat sac; the Ravager a walking fortress about 5 m long on six thick pillar legs, armored all over, with a crest of horns and glowing brood pods on its back.
  - **Spitter:** a beetle body with a swollen, glowing acid sac on its back; the acid glows in flight. Modeled close to the concept, like the mite and the burrower (see [Art direction](art.md#art-direction)).
- **Nests:** they live in organic nests / hives grown into the ground, from which they spawn. The ground around a nest can be covered by a living, slimy layer, which also marks their territory. Look: a low, wide mound of chitin and sinew with a glowing opening on top and tendrils spreading over the slime.
- **Escalation and attraction:** the longer a mission runs and the more the players mine, the more aliens come and the stronger the strains get; miners draw them in (see Nests and the hive above).
- **Readability:** silhouettes with clear head, legs and claws, glowing eyes or glands that show where they face, colors that don't clash with the ore colors and stand out on the ground: every strain glows in the swarmer's yellow-green (acid included), which no ore uses; stalkers have darker chitin. Death should read clearly too (collapse, splatter).
