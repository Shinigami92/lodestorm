# Quests

How the quests are built (the game's side: [Quests](../design/missions.md#quests); what's still planned: [`planned/quests.md`](../planned/quests.md)).

- **Data:** a `Quest` (`game/quests/quest.gd`) has a stable `id` (saved: never changed once released), a translated `title`, its `kind` (main, side, request), its `giver` (an `NpcProfile.id`), what it needs to be offered (`requires` flags, `requires_body`, `requires_reputation` with the giver; `is_open`), its `offer` (a `Topic` of the giver's, told before the offer card) and its `steps`. A `QuestStep` (`quest_step.gd`) has an `id`, the translated `text` of what it asks, its `goals`, who it's handed in at (`hand_in_to`, empty: the giver; `Quest.hand_in_npc`), its `hand_in` topic (its title is the entry in that NPC's list while the step is active, its lines are said after handing it in) and its rewards (`items` of ores and alloys, a `blueprint`, `reputation` with the giver). Every quest is listed in `game/quests/quests.tres` (a `QuestCatalog`), one `.tres` per quest with its steps, goals and topics built in, so `tools/translations.gd` (`Quest`, `QuestStep`, `Topic`) and `VoiceLines.speaking_resources` (`Quest` is in `VoiceLines.SPEAKING`: `Quest.spoken_lines` gives the offer's lines to the giver and each hand-in's to its NPC) find them by their header. `tests/quests/test_quest_content.gd` checks every quest: valid flags, unique ids, givers and hand-in NPCs that exist, topics named after who says them.
- **Goals** are Resources of their own on `QuestGoal` (`quest_goal.gd`: `describe`, `target`, and what changes their progress: `take_from` a haul, `count_run`, `is_built`, and `missing` for what the hold can pay at the hand-in): `DeliverGoal` (an ore or alloy and an amount; ores fill from the hauls, the rest is paid from the hold), `MineGoal` (an ore and an amount brought aboard in one run: the best run counts) and `BuildGoal` (a weapon owned, an upgrade at a level, or any crafted miner; counts what was built before the step started). A new kind is a new script and data. Goals done out in a region extend `RegionGoal` (`region_goal.gd`: the `region`'s `Region.id`, the `site` part it puts into a visit, how many quest `nests`): `ReachGoal` (a `site_part` `PartPlacement` and the translated `place` it names), `ClearNestsGoal` (an amount of quest nests) and `HuntGoal` (`hunt_goal.gd`: a `strain`, an `amount`, its `site_part` and the `nest_scene` of its brood nest, one of `game/aliens/nest/brood_*.tscn`; without one the strain is the hive's apex asleep in a lair and the target is 1, else `amount` and the nest, `counted` showing only the kills). `RecoverFragmentsGoal` (`recover_fragments_goal.gd`: an `amount` and an optional `site_part` with a crate, e.g. a wreck, so every visit has at least one) and `RecoverItemGoal` (`recover_item_goal.gd`: its `site_part`, the translated `item` it names and its look, `item_scene`, from `game/quests/items/`). `RegionGoal.hunted` and `brood_nest` tell the visit what to put at the site, `fragments` how many fragments go into the crates and `item_look` the item at the site; `QuestGoal.counted` is what the cards count of the target.
- **Visits:** `QuestVisit` (`quest_visit.gd`) is what the active steps put into one visit to a region, made by `Voyage` when it prepares a region's location (`GeneratedLocation.quest_visit`); its `key` says what it places, and when the quests change, the prepared locations whose key differs are prepared again (also the flight's destination, until it's reached). Each entry is one goal: a copy of its site's part (placed with the set pieces; `LocationParts.placed_for` finds it, and a `QuestSite` at its quest anchor reports the first player within 5 m) and its quest nests (`LocationSites` places them after the ordinary ones, from their own random stream; `AlienNest.kind` QUEST). A hunt's site gets, at its quest anchor, its brood nest (`AlienNest.kind` BROOD, `brood` its strain, `brood_left` from `QuestVisit.kills_left`; its kills `count_kill`, its destruction `count_brood`, which counts at `commit` once the kills are all done) or its strain asleep in an `ApexLair` (`asleep`: an `AlienSleep` child keeps `AlienStatus.asleep` until a player comes within its detection range or it's damaged; `AlienPose` lowers its body; a route of one point keeps it at the lair). `has_lair` keeps the location's own apex out. Its fragments (`fragment_entries`) go into the location's crates, one per crate, spread over them from their own random stream (`GeneratedLocation._crate_contents`: a `BlueprintFragment` holding its entry; as many as there are crates, the rest wait for a later visit); an item goal's site gets a `QuestItem` (an `Interactable` showing the item's look on an amber glow) at its anchor instead of a `QuestSite`, and a fragments goal's site none. Opening such a crate or picking the item up puts its entry into the player's `QuestPack` (`game/player/quest_pack.gd`, `Player.quest_pack`, following the backpack's CargoRack marker: a row of fragment slots below the tubes, items strapped across the bottom); `Player` drops it on death. When the player boards, `Ship` counts what they carry (`QuestVisit.count_carried`, only this visit's entries) before committing. What's done there is `count`ed in the visit and goes into the log (`QuestLog.add_progress`, only while that step is active, up to its target) when `Ship.player_boarded` fires or the run ends returned (`commit`); a lost run `drop`s the rest.
- **State:** the ship's `QuestLog` (`quest_log.gd`, `Ship.quests`, saved with the ship under `quests`: the accepted ids in order, the active step's progress per accepted quest, reputation per NPC up to `REPUTATION_MAX`, the `tracked` quest's id). Which step is active follows from the story's flags: the first one whose `quest.<quest>.<step>` flag isn't set (`current_step`); handing in the last one sets `quest.<quest>`. `offered_by(npc, reaches)` and `steps_at(npc)` give an NPC's quests, `quest_for(topic)` the quest a list entry belongs to. `Ship.store_haul` lets the active deliveries take what they need after the heat shield's plate (`take_from`, in the order accepted); `Ship` passes the bus's `mission_started` (`start_run`), `ore_hauled` (`count_hauled`) and the build events (`weapon_unlocked`, `upgrade_finished`, `miner_built`: `check_built` with the session's players) on. `hand_in(quest, payer, players)` takes what's missing from the payer's hold, sets the step's flag, pays the items and blueprint to every player in the session and the reputation to the giver, and starts the next step. A save naming a quest no longer in the catalog skips it. **Tracking:** `tracked` (`get_tracked`, `track`: only an accepted quest not done); `accept` sets it while it's empty, and handing in the tracked quest's last step moves it to the first active main quest (else empty).
- **At the NPC:** `Npc.get_choices` lists the offers and the steps handed in at them before the dialogue's topics; `Npc.get_quests` is the ship's log (a lone NPC has its own). The marker above their head (`NewMarker`) is a diamond while `has_quest_news` (a quest offered, or a step the player can hand in now), else round while `has_news`. A hand-in entry's dot (`Npc.is_new`) shows only while it can be handed in. `NpcTalk` tells an offer like a topic and then shows its card (`quest`, `offering`); a hand-in entry shows its card at once; `press` accepts or hands in (then `talk_about` its lines), `leave_topic` goes back to the list. The `TalkCard` shows a `QuestPanel` (`quest_panel.gd`: the step's text, the goals with their progress, what the hold pays of the rest as an `ItemCost`, the rewards as a `BadgeRow` and lines for a blueprint and reputation) with "Accept"/"Not now" or "Hand in" (disabled while it can't be)/"Back".
- **The story director:** `StoryDirector` (`game/story/story_director.gd`, `Ship.director`, saved with the ship under `director`) puts out `Beat`s (`game/story/beat.gd`, every one in `game/story/beats.tres`, a `BeatCatalog`): an `id` (its flag `beat.<id>`, set when it's put out: topics, quests and messages `require` it), the `npc` carrying it, `at` (a `CelestialBody.id` the ship must be at), `returns` (`ANY`, `RUN`, `DOCKING`), `priority`, `repeats` and `done_when` (the flag that ends its wait). Its `conditions` are `BeatCondition`s (`game/story/conditions/`: `FlagCondition`, `QuestCondition`, `ActCondition`, `RunsCondition`, `ShieldCondition` with `HeatShield.paid_share`, `OreFoundCondition`, `RegionVisitedCondition` by `Region.id`, `ReputationCondition`, `FallCondition`), each `holds(director, beat)`; a new kind is a new script and data. `Ship` passes `mission_started` (`start_run`: the region visited), `ore_hauled` (`find_ore`), `player_died` (`count_fall`), `mission_ended` (`end_run`) and `ShipFlight.docked` (`dock`, not a docking from a save) on, with the id of the body it's at. `direct(on, body)` puts out up to `per_return` beats (the one setting for "something on every return"): the highest priority among those `is_ready` (not put out yet unless it `repeats`, `Beat.fits` the return and the place, `Beat.holds`, nobody it's carried by `is_waiting`), while fewer than `MOST_WAITING` wait; `beat_put_out` tells who listens. `act()` is the heat shield's MK level, `runs_since(beat)` counts runs since a beat (any: `last_beat`). The debug overlay's "Story: next beat" puts one out now. `tests/story/test_story_director.gd` checks the picking, waiting, each condition, the save, and every beat in the catalog (ids, NPCs, places, waits).
- **Messages:** `MessageLog` (`game/messages/message_log.gd`, `Ship.messages`, saved with the ship under `messages`) holds what came in, oldest first (`received`, `ReceivedMessage`s: a `Message` and whether it's `heard`) and the waits that called already (`called`, their keys). A `Message` (`game/messages/message.gd`, every one in `game/messages/messages.tres`, a `MessageCatalog`) has an `id` (its flag `message.<id>`, set once it's heard), who it's `from` (an `NpcProfile.id`), its `lines` and the flags it `requires`; `VoiceLines.SPEAKING` lists it, so its lines are voiced by its sender. `StationWait.find_all(director)` (`game/messages/station_wait.gd`) lists who waits at a station: the director's waiting beats carried by someone with a `NpcProfile.station`, then the accepted quests' active steps handed in at someone there with every goal at its target (deliveries filled from the hauls), each with its `key` (the beat's flag, or the step's), the NPC, the station and the quest. `Ship` calls `come_in(body)` after the director on every return: the catalog's messages whose flags are set come in, and every wait not `called` yet calls (`ReceivedMessage.calling` with one of the NPC's `NpcProfile.calls`, at random but never the line of their last call), except at the station the ship is docked at (`body`), without call lines, or while an earlier call of theirs is unheard. `hear(entry)` marks it heard (a catalog message's flag set). The quest board's Messages tab (`QuestBoardStation.Tab.MESSAGES`, `BoardLists.message_lines`) plays one with its `BoardSpeaker` (`game/stations/quest_board/board_speaker.gd`, an `AudioStreamPlayer3D` on the Voice bus carrying like an NPC's voice): line by line, each for `VoiceLines.stay_time`, `hear` at the end. The navigator asks `MessageLog.waits()` every `NavigatorStation.WAITS_REFRESH` s for its diamonds (`NavigatorHologram.show_waits`) and the station card's "Waiting for you". The debug overlay's "Messages: a station call" puts a call on the board. `tests/messages/test_message_log.gd` checks coming in, hearing, the calls, the save and every message and call line; `tests/stations/test_quest_board.gd` the tab and playing.
- **The quest board:** `QuestBoardStation` (`game/stations/quest_board/quest_board.tscn`, on the bridge's back wall) is a station whose `Screen` (a `WorldPanel` with a `BoardScreen`: the tabs and `BoardScreen.LINES` lines, each with a `BoardMarker`, the title, a note and a step bar) shows `entries` (`BoardEntry`: a heading, a note, a quest, the done group or a job, with a `key` that keeps the selection on it when the list is made again) from `first` (the scroll). `BoardLists` makes them: `quest_lines` (groups by `Quest.Kind` in the order accepted, then the done group, open while `show_done`) and `job_lines` (the viewer's `Player.finished_jobs`, then their upgrade, skill, miner and furnace jobs under way by when they're done); `npc_name` and `place_of` read the giver from `NpcProfile.find` (its `station`: a `CelestialBody.id`, empty aboard). Its parts are made in code: one per tab and one at each line's right end (placed from the screen's `line_rect`, hidden where a line can't be selected); `step` walks the selectable entries (scrolling, `_select_entry`) and up to the tab, left and right and `_cycle` call `set_tab`, the wheel `scroll`s (`_shortcut`), the mouse picks through the screen's `pixel_at` (`_part_at`), and the station marks the selected and hovered line every frame (`BoardScreen.mark`). It makes the list again every `REFRESH` s (`update_list`). The `QuestBoardCard` beside the board (`_card_position`: level with the line, within the board's height) shows a quest (its `QuestPanel`, "Track": `track`), the done group ("Show"/"Hide": `toggle_done`), a job or the tab. Closing the board after its jobs were shown clears the user's `FinishedJobs` (`game/progression/finished_jobs.gd`: `FinishedJob`s noted from the player's `Upgrades.finished`, `Skills.finished`, `MinerFleet.built` and `part_finished`, and `Refining.batch_finished`, newest first, up to `MOST`, saved with the player under `finished`). `tests/stations/test_quest_board.gd` checks the groups, tracking, the jobs seen, walking and scrolling, and the screen and card with the most content in every language.
