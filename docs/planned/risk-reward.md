# Reasons to move on (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 14 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. The goal (see [Reasons to move and to stay](../design/missions.md#reasons-to-move-and-to-stay-risk-vs-reward)): standing at one spot until the timer runs low must not pay off. Ore nodes run dry (built, see [Miners](../design/mining.md)), every nest hollow has a rich field between its nests (built, see [Locations](../design/locations.md)) and a late deposit comes down far out (built, see [Missions](../design/missions.md#reasons-to-move-and-to-stay-risk-vs-reward)); carried ore is only safe once its carrier is aboard, which ends the mission when everyone alive is. Decided elsewhere: miners draw raiders and the hive frenzies in the storm's warning ([`aliens.md`](aliens.md)), quest and bonus goals elsewhere on the map ([`quests.md`](quests.md)), destroyable nests (built, see [Combat & aliens](../design/weapons.md)).

## Banking ore mid-run

A **cargo hatch** outside beside the ramp's foot: `interact` there unloads everything the player carries into the hold right away (safe from dying) without going aboard, so the mission goes on, solo players too. It costs the walk back, which is the decision: bank now, or push further with everything on the back. The ore flows into the hatch like through a hose (greybox about 2 s); the hold's silos fill as usual. Quest items stay carried (they count aboard).
