# Combat and weapons

- Aliens outside the ship try to kill the player; player fights back with weapons.
- **Weapons:** a fixed set of unique weapons, ranged and melee, each with its own in-game name (untranslated, like the ores and places) and a plain class word under it on the cards (translated: "Harl · Assault rifle"); all 18 (see [Weapons](#weapons)). New ones come as blueprints from quests and the story and are built at the workbench (see [Upgrade stations](progression.md#upgrade-stations)). Ranged weapons use **ammo** made from carried ore (see [Ammo and reloading](#ammo-and-reloading)). The player can't carry all of them: before a mission they pick a **loadout** of a few weapons in the ship and play the mission with it.
- **No drops:** aliens drop nothing; ore only comes from mining. Killing is about safety, not loot, so the miners stay central.
- **Attacking:** one attack button; the equipped weapon decides what it does (shoot, swing). Melee stays on it: a separate melee button would make the melee weapons pointless.
- **Loadout slots take any weapon type**, so a melee-only loadout is a real choice. Melee is never required to get on in a run (nothing that only a melee weapon can do).
- **Starting weapons:** a new player owns Harl (ranged) and Dray (melee), and both are in the loadout. The others come as blueprints through the game (from the quests, step 16; until then the debug overlay's "Give blueprints"); the first one to find is Brask; Pinner, Grindle, Quill, Tremor, Wick, Scald, Kith, Zenith, Fulgen, Nadir, Riven, Auger, Jag, Clout and Mantle are blueprints too. Owned weapons and the loadout belong to each player (see [Co-op progression](progression.md#co-op-progression)).

## Weapons

**18 weapons**, 12 ranged and 6 melee: two to start with, then about three per planet, so every planet brings ranged and melee ones (fewer melee weapons, since ranged ones have more ways to differ). The count is data: the armory, the workbench and the tests work for any number. **Three origins** shape their look and group them on the racks: the **colony arms** (the militia's standard issue, plain and sturdy), the **converted mining tools** (the prospectors' tradition: industrial, weathered, hazard paint) and the **expedition prototypes** (built by the lost expedition from signature ores and found along its trail: odd and glowing). Each name has its own first letter; Nadir and Zenith, the expedition's first and last prototype, are a pair on purpose.

- **Against the aliens:** weapons work with two alien values, **armor** and **stagger resistance** (see [Aliens](aliens.md)). Effects instead of damage types: burn, shock, knockback, pull, slow. No elemental weaknesses and nothing only one weapon can kill: each strain has weapons that are better against it, never required ones.
- **Solo and co-op:** no weapon is dead weight alone (Kith repairs miners, Mantle shields miners, Nadir sets up the player's own second weapon); co-op combos (Nadir with Tremor, Mantle in front of Quill) are a bonus.
- **Ammo colors** come from a warm palette (amber, orange, gold, warm white), shared by several weapons and never an ore's hue, so shots never read as ore.
- **Composed of Resources** (`game/weapons/`): a weapon (`WeaponType`, one folder per weapon in `game/weapons/types/`) is its **feed** (its ammo: magazines, single rounds, a battery or a fuel cell that heats up; none for melee weapons, but a shield's field charge, which isn't ammo), its **trigger** (how holding the attack button attacks: at its rate while held, one attack per press, barrels or a blade spinning up first, a charge let go, or a press to thrust and holding on to drill), its **delivery** (how an attack reaches its targets: projectiles, which can pierce, a shell lobbed in an arc that blasts where it lands, a charge tossed onto the ground, a cone of flame, a beam, lightning jumping from alien to alien, an orb opening a gravity well, a swing (also a chain of slashes ending in a heavy one, a saw's cuts and a shield's bash), a thrust through a line that can drill into what it hit, or a punch at the nearest alien), its **effects** (what a hit does: damage with stagger and push, scaled by a partial charge, setting aliens burning, slowing them, throwing them, healing teammates and repairing miners), a shield's **guard** (its field), its upgrade **parts**, its origin (colony arms, converted mining tools, expedition prototypes), its draw time and a greybox scene, so most new weapons are data. `all_weapons.tres` lists every weapon; the tests go over all of them.
- **First weapons** (values **TBD (playtest)**): **Harl** (assault rifle, colony) fires fast single shots (8 a second) that reach far; **Brask** (shotgun, colony) fires a fan of pellets (7 over 30°) that only reaches 12 m; **Dray** (hammer, converted tool) swings at everything within 2.5 m in a 120° arc in front of the player (also several aliens at once), unless something solid is in the way, about 1.5 times a second; it comes down as a two-handed overhead blow (from over the right shoulder, slammed down in front, the face of its upright head leading).
- **Colony arms 1** (values **TBD (playtest)**):
  - **Pinner** (rivet pistol): weak shots (6 damage), one per press of the attack button (holding fires no more), up to 6 a second, 20 m. Its battery holds 12 shots and needs no ore: 0.8 s after the last shot it recharges by itself (5 shots a second); run empty, it's drained and recharges fully in 2.5 s before it fires again. No spares, no reload by hand, and a teammate can't hand it ammo. The ranged fallback when the ore runs out.
  - **Grindle** (rotary gun): holding the attack button spins its barrels up for 0.6 s (no shots), then it fires 14 shots a second (6 damage, 22 m) while held. Let go, the barrels keep spinning for 1 s, so a quick press fires at once, then spin down within 0.6 s. The player walks slower while they spin (60 % at full speed). A belt of 120 rounds (0.15 ore units a round) and a long reload (3.5 s, window 55–67 %, a miss +0.8 s).
  - **Quill** (rail rifle): holding the attack button charges the shot (1.2 s to full, only with a round loaded), letting go fires it, and switching weapons loses it. A partial charge does 30–100 % of the damage (90 at full, with its stagger and push) and stops at the first alien; a full charge pierces every alien in its line (rock and nests stop it). While it charges, a line from the muzzle shows where the shot goes (through aliens, up to the first rock or nest), brighter as it charges and white when full. A fast slug (90 m/s, 35 m), 5 rounds (1 ore unit each), a 2 s reload, at most 2.5 shots a second.
- **Converted mining tools 1** (values **TBD (playtest)**):
  - **Tremor** (seismic launcher): lobs a shell in a high arc (the higher the farther, so it clears rocks and cliffs; under a ceiling, as in the shooting range, it stays flat enough to pass beneath it) that blasts where it lands: on the locked alien or nest, else at the spot the mouse cursor points at (also on a rock's top), else 12 m along the aim (the stick, a VR hand); at most 22 m. It lands early on rock, the ground or an alien in its way. While it's out, a ring on the ground in its ammo color shows where the next shell lands and how wide its blast is (only for its holder). 70 damage with a strong stagger and push, 1.2 shells a second while held, 4 rounds (2 ore units each), a 2.6 s reload, a 3.5 m blast.
  - **Wick** (charge layer): one press tosses a proximity charge a short way, past the aliens in between (where Tremor would land, at most 6 m, the stick 4 m), with the same ring showing where it lands and how far it reaches. It settles on the ground, arms after 1 s (two low beeps, its light on and pulsing), and an alien within 2.5 m trips it: it flashes for 0.35 s (fast beeps) and blasts (90 damage, a 3 m blast). Players and miners never trip it. A faint ring in its layer's player color shows its reach. A player has at most 3 out at once; one more removes their oldest. They stay until they blast or the mission ends. 6 charges per magazine (1.5 ore units each), a 2.2 s reload, at most 2 tosses a second.
  - **The blast** (`Blast`, both): every alien within it takes the hit, the full one at the middle and down to half at the edge, pushed away from the middle; rock in between shelters it; a nest whose edge is within it takes it too, and what a shell hits directly takes the full blast. No friendly fire: players and miners are never hurt. It flashes, throws up dust and shakes the view of players nearby.
- **Heat weapons** (values **TBD (playtest)**): they fire continuously while the attack button is held (10 attacks a second, one round of their fuel cell each) from a fuel cell that is their magazine (spares first, then ore, reloaded like a magazine with the timing window). Every attack adds heat; 0.5 s after letting go (Zenith 0.6 s) it cools by itself, so short bursts never overheat. At full heat it **overheats** (a ping and an alarm): it stops firing and vents for 2 s (Zenith 2.5 s), its weapon's nozzle, fins or rings glowing hot. `reload` while it's hot (40 % or more) vents it early, for that share of the time, else it swaps the cell; during a vent the press is a timing press like a reload's (within the window: cool at once; outside: 0.5 s longer). Their flame or beam shows while they fire, for teammates too, with its loop sound.
  - **Scald** (thermal cutter, converted tool): a cone of flame in front of the player, 5.5 m long and 40° wide (closer than 1.2 m anything in front of the player), around the aim or towards the locked target. Every alien in it takes a hit (4 damage, a little stagger) ten times a second, unless rock is between it and the nozzle (other aliens, players and miners don't shelter it); a nest whose edge is in it too. What it hits **burns** (`Burning`: flames on the alien or nest, 8 damage a second past armor, counted in half-second steps) for 3 s after the last hit; a new flame refreshes it (the stronger, the longer), burns don't add up or spread, and an alien burrowing puts it out. Heat full after 5 s of fire; a cell of 80 (0.08 ore units each), a 2 s reload.
  - **Kith** (mender, expedition prototype): a beam from its emitter along the aim (or at the locked target), 12 m, that sticks to the first thing it touches: a teammate heals (2 health per hit, 20 a second), a miner is repaired by as much (a broken one too, until it mines again), an alien takes a weak hit (1.5 damage); rock and nests stop it. No healing of its holder; reviving downed teammates comes with co-op (M7). Heat full after 8 s; a cell of 100 (0.06 ore units each), a 2 s reload. Solo it's the miners' repair tool at range; the debug overlay's "Hurt teammates" takes half the health of the teammates it adds, to try it.
  - **Zenith** (star lance, expedition prototype, the campaign's last weapon): a beam 25 m along the aim (or at the locked target) through every alien in its line, past players and miners (no friendly fire), stopped by rock and nests. 8 damage a hit, rising the longer it stays on the same alien, up to three times after 2 s; leaving an alien starts it over. The beam grows brighter and wider as it heats up on its target. Heat full after 4 s; a cell of 60 (0.15 ore units each), a 2.4 s reload.
- **Expedition prototypes 2** (values **TBD (playtest)**):
  - **Fulgen** (arc caster): while the attack button is held, 4 arcs of lightning a second, each to the locked target within 14 m (an alien, or a nest: the arc ends there), else to the nearest alien within 14 m and 60° around the aim, fliers too (lightning goes to the alien itself; aliens hidden by a stalker's cloak aren't picked, like the target lock), then jumping on up to 3 times to the nearest alien within 5 m of the last one that it hasn't hit yet. 15 damage and a strong stagger (20, so a second arc staggers a swarmer and breaks off a pounce or a scream), each jump 75 % of the hit before. Rock stops an arc, aliens, players and miners don't. With no alien to reach it still fires: the arc strikes the ground 8 m along the aim, or what's in the way (a nest takes the hit). A jagged warm-white bolt along its path, flickering for a moment, with a flash at its prongs. A magazine of 30 (0.2 ore units each), a 2 s reload.
  - **Nadir** (gravity projector): one press fires a slow, dark orb (12 m/s) where Tremor would land (the locked alien or nest, else the cursor's spot, else 12 m along the aim; at most 20 m), flying almost straight and through aliens; rock, a nest or the ground stop it, and a gold ring on the ground shows where it opens and how far it pulls (only for its holder). There it opens a **gravity well** (`GravityWell`): every alien within 5 m with no rock in between is pulled to its middle within about half a second (lighter strains faster, heavy ones like the warden and the brute slower) and **held** there for 3 s: it can't walk, leap, charge or dive (one under way is broken off), only attacks that don't move it (a bite in reach, a spit) still come, fliers sink to 1 m, in reach of every weapon, and burrowers can't dig in; the Ravager isn't held, only slowed to half its speed. Aliens walking into it are caught too. Then it **bursts** like a blast (25 damage and a strong stagger, nests in reach too, rock shelters) without a push, so the clump stays together a moment for the other weapons. Players and miners are never affected. It shows a dark core in a pulsing gold glow, its edge and rings drawn inwards on the ground, flickering faster in its last second, and hums. A player has one well open: the next shot bursts the last one at once. Its battery holds 2 wells and needs no ore: 1 s after the last shot it recharges one well every 6 s, and running empty doesn't drain it (it fires again as soon as a well is back). Nadir does little damage itself; it sets up the second weapon in the loadout (Tremor, Wick, Scald, Brask).
- **Converted mining tools 2, melee** (values **TBD (playtest)**):
  - **Riven** (blade): while the attack button is held, 3 slashes a second, each hitting every alien within 1.8 m in a narrow 60° arc (12 damage, a small stagger), sweeping across from the right to the left and staying there for a moment, so the next slash of the chain sweeps back from that side. Slashes within 0.6 s of each other make a chain, and the third is a **heavy slash**: the same arc, twice a slash's damage and push, and four times its stagger, so it staggers a swarmer. A longer pause starts the chain over. The player stays where they are.
  - **Auger** (drill lance): one press thrusts 4 m straight ahead (at most 1.2 thrusts a second): every alien in a narrow line (0.6 m wide) takes 20 damage with a solid stagger; rock stops it, and a nest in the line takes it. Holding on after a thrust that hit **drills** into the first alien or nest it hit: 10 hits a second, each a quarter of a thrust's damage at first and rising to three times that over 2 s, so armor takes less off; the player walks at 40 % meanwhile and the bit spins faster. The drill lets go when its target dies, digs in or gets beyond 4 m, and when the button is let go.
  - **Jag** (rock saw): holding the attack button spins its blade up within 0.15 s, then it cuts every alien within 2 m and 100° in front ten times a second while held (4 damage, no push). Each cut adds stagger (10), so swarmers, mites and shriekers stay staggered in it while heavy strains fill slowly, and everything it touches is **slowed** to half its speed until a quarter second after the last cut. The player walks at half speed while the blade spins. For holding a choke point.
- **Expedition prototypes 3, melee** (values **TBD (playtest)**):
  - **Clout** (power fist): holding the attack button charges a punch (0.9 s to full; the player walks at 70 % meanwhile, the gauntlet's pistons draw back and its core glows brighter, white when full, with a ping), letting go punches (at most 1.5 a second; a quick press is a jab at 30 % of the power). The punch lands on one alien only, the nearest within 2.2 m and 60° in front with no rock in between (or a nest whose edge is nearer): 60 damage and a strong stagger (60). It **throws** the alien along the aim: up to 7 m at a full charge, 1.5 m for a jab, times the strain's knockback (the warden about 2 m, the brute 1.4 m; the Ravager isn't thrown). It flies at 16 m/s through players, miners and other aliens, staggered, and stays staggered for 0.5 s after it lands; a pounce, charge or wind-up under way is broken off, and a punch takes an alien out of a gravity well (the well may pull it back in). Every alien within 0.8 m of its way takes half the punch (damage and stagger) and is knocked 2 m aside; they don't fly on. Flying into rock or a nest stops it there with half the punch again on top (the nest takes the half share too), staggered for 1 s, a brute dazed like after its charge into rock (1.5 s, its back open). The player stays where they are.
  - **Mantle** (shield): a plate on the left forearm whose **field** stands while it's drawn: a curved, see-through pane of warm light 1.1 m in front of the player, 120° around where they face and 2.2 m high, turning with them; the player walks at 85 %. It stops every alien blow coming from within its arc (bites, strikes, talon slashes, pounces, dives, the charges and slams) and every alien shot that crosses it (spitter and skimmer acid, flesh hooks), whoever it was aimed at, so teammates and miners right behind it are covered too; a glob lobbed high over it still comes down behind. Blows from the sides or behind, splashes and puddles behind it, acid clouds and burning ground aren't stopped, and players' own shots pass it. Each stop drains its **charge** (100) by the damage; 1.5 s after the last one it recharges (25 a second). A hit bigger than what's left goes through with the rest and **breaks** the field: it's down for 3 s, then back full. A heavy blow it stops (a brute's or the Ravager's charge, a warden's or the Ravager's slam) also shoves the player 2 m back, and a charge it stops ends with the charger dazed as if it ran into rock. One press **bashes**: every alien within 1.8 m in the field's arc takes 10 damage, 40 stagger (a swarmer is staggered, a pounce or a scream broken off) and a strong push (8 m/s), at most 1.2 a second; the field blocks during the shove, but not for 0.3 s after it while the arm comes back. The field dims as its charge runs down, flares and sends a ring out where it's hit and hums quietly while it stands; its charge shows on the plate's six cells and on the suit ring's ammo arc like a battery (it's no ammo: no ore, no reload, nothing to hand over).
- **Loadout slots:** 2 (`PlayerSettings.loadout_slots`, **TBD (playtest)**). `switch_weapon` (Q, gamepad Y / Triangle) equips the weapon in the next filled slot, also during a mission, and draws it: the weapon swings up from the hip and can't attack for its draw time (greybox: Pinner 0.2 s, Harl and Riven 0.3 s, Brask, Wick, Kith and Clout 0.4 s, Quill, Scald, Fulgen and Auger 0.5 s, Dray, Tremor, Nadir, Jag and Mantle 0.6 s, Zenith 0.7 s, Grindle 0.8 s); switching cancels a reload and a charge.

## Ammo and reloading

Values in `game/weapons/*_settings.tres` and `game/player/player_settings.tres`, all **TBD (playtest)**.

- **Magazines:** each ranged weapon fed with ore holds a magazine (greybox: Harl 24 rounds, Brask 6 shells, Grindle a belt of 120, Quill 5, Tremor 4, Wick 6 charges, Fulgen 30 arcs; the heat weapons' fuel cells Scald 80, Kith 100, Zenith 60); a shot uses one round (a heat weapon's attack too, ten a second). Pinner and Nadir run on a battery instead (see Pinner and Nadir above). A magazine upgrade holds more (see [Upgrade stations](progression.md#upgrade-stations)). Melee weapons need no ammo, so they're the fallback when the ore runs out; nothing in a run needs ammo.
- **Spare magazines first:** each ranged weapon in the loadout starts a run with 2 spare magazines (`PlayerSettings.spare_magazines`; Brask: 2 loads of shells), so with the loaded one that's 3. Reloads take them first, for free; a Harl reload with a spare still throws away the rounds left in the old magazine. More spares are a skill.
- **Then made from carried ore:** once the spares are used, a reload pays ore from the player's cargo for each round it loads, so the carried ore is the reserve. Any ore type works, so it never depends on what a location has; the commonest one is used first (lowest tier: Kyrite, then Olvane, ...), so rare ore is spent last, and rarer ores make more ammo per unit (greybox: one Kyrite unit makes 4 Harl rounds or 1 Brask shell; Olvane and Tessar 1.25×, mid ores 1.5×, signature ores 2×). Aboard (also in the shooting range) reloads are free, from the ship's stock.
- **Full for every run:** each run starts with full magazines and spares, and so does respawning in the medical bay (health carries over, see [Death & persistence](death-and-persistence.md)). Without ore, those three magazines are all there is until the miners deliver, so mining early or taking a melee weapon still matters.
- **Reloading** (`reload`): by hand at any time, and by itself when the magazine runs empty (also when pulling the trigger on an empty weapon once ore is carried again). Without ore to pay for it, nothing happens and the gauge blinks red. The player walks slower while reloading (greybox: 75 % speed).
  - **Harl, Grindle, Quill, Tremor, Wick, Fulgen, Scald, Kith, Zenith (magazine, a fuel cell):** a reload throws away the rounds left in the magazine and loads a full one (Harl 1.6 s, Grindle 3.5 s, Quill 2 s, Tremor 2.6 s, Wick 2.2 s, Fulgen, Scald and Kith 2 s, Zenith 2.4 s; a hot heat weapon vents instead, see Heat weapons), paying for all of it; with too little ore it loads what the ore pays, and it doesn't start when that would be no more than what's loaded. It can't fire meanwhile.
  - **Brask (single shells):** loads one shell after another (0.5 s each) until it's full or the ore runs out; the loaded shells stay. Pulling the trigger stops the reload and fires what's loaded.
  - **Switching weapons** cancels a reload: Harl's magazine stays empty (it was thrown away), Brask keeps the shells it loaded. The rounds of a holstered weapon stay as they are for the run.
- **Timing reload:** pressing `reload` again during a reload is a timing press. Within the window (Harl: 55–70 % of the reload; Brask: 40–90 % of each shell, so it opens late enough to react to the next shell after a hit) the reload (the shell) finishes right away; outside it, it takes longer (Harl +0.5 s, Brask +0.3 s per shell), and there's only one press per reload (shell). Not pressing is normal speed, so it's an optional skill, never required. Brask's shells timed in a row load quicker (5 % each, up to 10 %); a miss or a shell without a press breaks the chain. The window opening ticks (a sound cue, needed for VR and accessibility, see [Sound](sound.md)), and a press sounds whether it hit.
- **Shown in the world:** on the weapon, Harl's six counter segments each light up for a sixth of the magazine (Pinner's six cell segments, Grindle's ten on its drum and Fulgen's ten cells likewise), and Brask's strip has a glowing shell per round (Quill's, Tremor's and Wick's cells too, and a cell per well on Nadir; the heat weapons' eight cells each a share of the fuel); the last segment (shell) blinks amber when it's nearly empty, all of them blink red when it's empty and nothing pays a reload. During a reload the counter fills up like a bar with the window's segment white (red after a miss); the shell being loaded brightens, white within the window. Since the weapon is small at camera distance, the suit ring's ammo arc shows the same (see [UI](ui.md)), and it flashes red when a reload can't start (not with a full magazine: then `reload` just does nothing).
- **Handing a teammate ammo** (co-op support role): holding `interact` next to a teammate (within 2 m, for 0.8 s, with no terminal or button in reach) gives them a magazine for their equipped weapon, paid from the giver's ore, by the same rules as a reload (a magazine replaces theirs, shells top it up). The suit ring's outer band fills green meanwhile. Works with local players now; over the network with co-op (M7).
- **Spares shown on the ring:** the equipped weapon's spare magazines are ticks inside the suit ring's ammo arc, in the weapon's ammo color; a partly used one (Brask's shells) is dimmer, used ones are only an outline. They show on a reload and when a spare is used, then fade.
- **Skills** (see [Progression](progression.md)): more spare magazines, keeping the rounds left in a magazine when reloading, reloading at full walking speed (relevant once aliens come in faster and slower variants), the suit rebuilding spare magazines from carried ore during a run. Maybe a reload speed or a wider window as an upgrade part, if playtests ask for it.
