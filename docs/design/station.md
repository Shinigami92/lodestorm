# Stations and outposts

- **Stations and outposts:** besides the ship, the players can visit stations and outposts in the system: handcrafted places to walk around in, with NPCs, story and quests, without enemies. **Waypost Ostra**, the colony's hub, orbits Torvane; the ship docks there from the start (no tier), and it never gates progress. Planned: the station NPCs' requests and deliveries with the quests (see [`planned/station.md`](../planned/station.md)); a second station and inhabited outposts later.
  - **Docking** (values **TBD (playtest)**): the navigator picks the station like a region (see [Mission selection](missions.md#mission-selection)) and the flight console sets course. The flight crosses space like a flight to another body, the station growing behind the hull, and ends without clouds: the station glides in from the right until the ship's tail reaches through the open pressure frame in its hangar's left wall (its opening fits the tail with the nacelles), and four docking clamps close on the hull's lower sides under the nacelles (greybox: 14 s in space, 18 s docking with the clamps in its last 4 s, the camera jolting as they take hold; from a location the take-off comes first). The ramp then leads (to the right, as always) down onto the hangar's deck. There's no timer, storm or mission: the ramp button lowers and raises the ramp freely, and weapons stay holstered in the station too. The countdown above the door says "Docked".
  - **Leaving:** pick a destination at the navigator and set course at the flight console; that needs everyone aboard ("Wait until everyone is back aboard"), and the ramp goes up by itself. The ship undocks (the clamps let go, the station drifts off behind the hull, greybox 10 s) and flies on. Where the ship is gets saved: a save made while docked loads docked, the station around the ship. A new game still starts in space.
  - **The map** (greybox, built from data like the ship's hull, sizes **TBD (playtest)**): one deck around a central hall, compact (the gallery's far wall about 150 m from the ramp, about 25 s on foot). The station's rooms follow the ship's camera rules, cut away further since they're wide: while the viewer is inside the station or aboard the docked ship, its roofs and every wall but the back ones only cast shadows, leaving low stubs, so the rooms stay outlined. Room names are painted on the floors by the doors, and amber floor guides lead from the ramp to the rooms and back to the hangar. Big windows in the back walls look onto Torvane below; outside, solar wings and a mast. Each room has its own floor tint and warm work lights:

    ```text
    +---------+-----------+-----------+----------+
    |         |  DEPOT    |  COUNCIL  |          |
    |         +-----=-----+-----=-----+          |
    = HANGAR  =     CONCOURSE (hall)  = GALLERY  |
    |         +-----=-----+           |          |
    |         |   BAR     |  (hall)   |          |
    +---------+-----------+-----------+----------+
    ```

    The **hangar** (48 × 56 m, 18 m high; the ship's tail and its ramp, a side door to the depot), the **concourse** (the hall, 10 m high, reaching to the front beside the bar), the **supply depot**, the **council office** (one big window), the **bar** and the **observation gallery** at the far end (tall panes side by side along its back wall). Furniture (greybox): the depot's counter in front of its shelves, the council's long table with chairs, the bar's counter along its left wall and two booths on its right (a table between two benches with backrests), three stalls along the front of the concourse (a counter and a rack: scenery, no shops), two benches in the gallery facing the windows; the ore exchange's two hoppers on the depot's counter.
  - **Its people** (values **TBD (playtest)**): four named NPCs with the crew's template (name plate, the amber marker, talking), each explaining their place and none naming locked content: **Kade**, the depot master, beside the depot's counter (the depot, the exchange, the colony, about Kade); **Selin**, the council member, at the head of the long table (the council, Torvane, about Selin); **Nell**, the bartender, behind the bar's counter, talked to across it (close to it, so the talk stays framed tight) (the bar, about Nell, and one topic of rumors per planet or moon the ship reaches, a line per region about its set piece, so a new one opens with every heat shield plate); **Rook**, the captain of a rival prospecting crew, by a booth in the bar (prospecting, staying alive, about Rook). What's heard is saved with the ship, like the crew's topics. They stand at their places on every docking (their places glide in with the station) and know their own room like the crew (the depot, the council office, the bar), with a line for elsewhere where one points at it (Selin's "this table") and lines that fit anywhere otherwise (Kade's "stored in my depot"); their greetings don't need them, they're heard before anyone leaves their room. **They walk around** (values **TBD (playtest)**): every docking they start at their places; until their greeting is heard (once per save) they stay in their own room, then they move on every 20–40 s like the crew, walking around the furniture on the station's own navigation mesh: Kade between his counter, the depot's shelves and its door to the hangar (watching crates come in); Selin between the head of the table and the window, now and then out to the observation gallery or the concourse; Nell mostly behind her counter (also further along it, wiping it and polishing glasses, talked to across it there too), now and then at the booth with the two guests, wiping its table, clearing a glass and looking around, where she's talked to at her feet; Rook at his booth and at the bar's counter, now and then in the hangar looking over the docked ship or at the gallery's windows. Outside their own room they only stand and look around; at their places they also stretch and check their wrist now and then (the crew's shared clips). Talked to while walking, they stop and talk where they stand. The bar never closes yet (see [`planned/npc-places.md`](../planned/npc-places.md)). Nell has her own model, close to her concept: a sturdy woman in her forties with a brown braid, a slate work jacket with rolled sleeves, tattooed forearms, fingerless gloves, a stained canvas apron on leather straps, a belt with a holster, cargo trousers with knee pads and lace-up boots, standing and breathing, walking with her own stride (her body and face made with MakeHuman, what she wears and her hair generated, see [`technical/characters.md`](../technical/characters.md), People). She stands close behind the counter and works while no player is within 3 m: now and then (3–8 s of standing between, never the same twice in a row; values **TBD (playtest)**) she wipes the counter, polishes a glass, or crouches for a bottle, looks at its label and puts it on the low back bar behind her right shoulder; when a player comes close she stops and turns to them. The glass, the bottle and the rag are their own models. The other three have their own models too, each with the generated head of their concept (it doesn't blink or move its lips): Kade, a broad, heavy-set man in his fifties with a graying beard, in a stained pale work jacket and overalls with orange high-visibility stripes and thick black gloves; Selin, a slim woman in her fifties with short dark hair, in a long pale coat worn open over a dark quilted uniform; Rook, a lean man in his thirties with short messy hair, stubble and a grin, in a scuffed pale armored mining suit over a dark quilted undersuit, a crew patch with a star on the chest plate (his helmet under the arm comes later). Their work at their places comes later (see [`planned/people.md`](../planned/people.md)).
  - **The ore exchange** (values **TBD (playtest)**): a safety valve at a bad rate, not a shop. Two square steel hoppers on Kade's counter, the left one taking ore in, the right one giving it out, each with a plate on the counter's front below it ("In"/"Out" and the ore picked). `interact` in front of the counter opens it like a station, with the hoppers as its parts (the directions move between them); one card over the counter shows both: what goes in (any ore the player's own hold has at least 3 of, signature ores too) and what comes out (a common or mid ore the hold ever had, never the one going in; signature ores can be traded away but never bought, so a planet's signature ore is still mined there, and ores not found yet stay unknown), each row with arrows to pick the ore (`tab_prev`/`tab_next` pick the selected hopper's), its amount and what the hold has; then how much comes out (-10/-5/-1, +1/+5/+10 or `zoom_in`/`zoom_out`, up to what the hold pays for), the rate (3 in, 1 out), "Exchange" and "Close". A trade is paid from the player's own hold at once (in co-op each player trades from theirs), and the amount stays for the next one (at most what's left pays for); both hoppers flare in their ore's color with a clatter of ore. Without an ore to put in, or another one to get, a line on the card says why.
  - **Ambient colonists:** twelve, each their own person with a role on the plate and no name (a mechanic and a barter trader at the stalls, a hydroponics gardener browsing, a dock hand at the bar's counter, a depot clerk at the shelves; sitting: a hauler pilot on a gallery bench looking out of the windows, a water technician and a metalworker facing each other in the booth by the bar's counter after their shift; and walking: a cargo loader in the hangar, a courier from the hangar to the gallery, a maintenance technician in the concourse, a teacher around the gallery), in the same suits in worn tints with dim collar lights. Seats are 0.45 m high, the sitter's feet in front of them; a seated colonist sits from the start, doesn't turn to a player or get up, and its remark bubble sits above its lower head. A walker may sit down on its way: the teacher sits on the other gallery bench for 20 s (round its end, on the window side), turning to the windows, then gets up and walks on (sitting down and getting up blend over 0.8 s; values **TBD (playtest)**). Walkers follow set routes in a loop (straight between waypoints, stopping 3 s at each) with a walking stride of their own, and stop while a player is within 3 m, turning to them like the crew. When a player stops within 2 m, they say a remark of their own (five each, about their work, none shared with another colonist) in a speech bubble that stays until the player walks off (2.6 m); the next one comes when a player comes back. The bubble grows with the camera's view, so it reads like a crew member's while talking. They can't be talked to.
