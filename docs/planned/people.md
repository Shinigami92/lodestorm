# People's own models (planned)

Decided 2026-10-24 (a generated outfit over the body since 2026-10-25), built in parts with the art pass (M5, see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. The way people are made, Nell (the first), Kade, Selin and Rook (with their generated heads) are built (see [`TECHNICAL.md`](../TECHNICAL.md#4-asset-pipeline), People, and Waypost Ostra in [`GAME_DESIGN.md`](../GAME_DESIGN.md)); what's left:

- **Everyone the same way as at Waypost Ostra:** an A-pose concept re-rendered from their rated concept, its TRELLIS.2 outfit and hair over a MakeHuman body with their sliders, each a short build script; the crew next (their looks are decided and their concepts rated, see [`crew.md`](crew.md)); the colonists last, a few bodies with mixed genders and ages (the batch-8 concepts were all men) in a few outfits, at about 15k triangles each.
- **Hard parts that shouldn't bend:** a generated outfit bends with the body under it everywhere; where that shows (plates, helmets, a holster swinging with a thigh), they're cut out and put on one bone each.
- **Work at their place:** everyone gets a few activities of their own, like Nell's at the bar (Kade at the depot's counter, Selin at the council's table, the crew at their posts), with the props they need as their own models.
- **Held props:** Kade's slate, Selin's tablet and Rook's helmet under his arm come with talk gestures, as accessories on a hand bone; the A-pose concepts leave the hands empty.
- **Selin's long coat:** below the hips it blends into both thighs like Nell's apron, so it holds up if she ever walks.
- **The backs:** the generator guesses a person's back from the front view (Nell's apron shows from behind); a concept from behind as a second view where it matters.
- **Assets:** CC0 by default; a CC-BY asset only when nothing CC0 fits, recorded with its author in `assets/CREDITS.md`, which the store page and the game's credits read from.
- **Hair:** the generated hair cap for now (fine from the game's camera). Looked at 2026-10-25: strands simulated on the GPU (a TressFX port for Godot 4.7, MIT) would cost about 1–2 ms and 45 MB per hairstyle and need hair as strand curves; worth a test on one person for VR's close views (M6), behind a quality setting. Cheaper first: a hair shader on the generated hair (a sheen along the strands, darker roots). Research pipelines and datasets under non-commercial licenses (CT2Hair, the assets of GodotHair) are out.
- **Faces later:** a neutral face for now (no blinking, no mouth); a generated head (Kade's and Selin's, the user's pick 2026-10-25: it looks like the concept, MakeHuman's face looked young and generic on them; women's faces come out softer from the generator, Selin's best of four seeds) stays still for good, unless it's remade on a MakeHuman face later (its shape and colors projected onto it); MakeHuman's expression and mouth shapes (visemes) stay out of the export until lip sync and blinking get their own round, with the voice lines.
- **Fighting people:** every person has the same skeleton, so the player's weapon and melee clips can be baked for an NPC who fights (a mission situation), with the IK targets fitted to their body.
- **The player's suit:** the suit's hard parts (plates, helmet, backpack, canisters) split off the generated mesh or remade, each on one bone, only the cloth between them skinned; the visor its own glossy surface reflecting the scene instead of the reflection painted into its texture; later a MakeHuman body under the armor, so the player shares the skeleton with everyone.
- **Budget:** about 48k triangles for a named person (Nell: the outfit 39k, what's left of the body 8k; the outfit generated at 80k faces and 4096 textures for the talk's close view), the skin 2048, the rest 1024; to look at again when many people are on screen at once (a lower level of detail from afar).
