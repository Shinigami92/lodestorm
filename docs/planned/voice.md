# Voice-over (planned: the rest)

Decided 2026-10-17. All values **TBD (playtest)**. The model (Qwen3-TTS, picked in the spike), the pipeline, the Voice and Radio buses with the radio filter, the voice language setting and playback from the speech bubbles and the radio bubble are built, with Brannik, Nell, Kade, Selin and the crew voiced (see [Localization and voice](../design/localization-and-voice.md), and [`technical/voice.md`](../technical/voice.md) for the tools, the candidates and how to write a voice's description). What's left:

- **The rest of the cast:** the crew as they come and the colonists' remarks (they speak when their bubble shows). The crew and the station's named NPCs get voices picked by ear, each from its own rounds (a voice the ratings kept as a spare is a starting point). Colonists and other minor NPCs (decided 2026-10-25) don't need a recognizable voice: one is picked for each without rounds of its own (a spare, or a take designed from a short description of the person), but each keeps its anchor, so a colonist always sounds like themselves, in both languages. About 15 voices in the end (8 crew, 4 station NPCs, a few spares), clearly apart in pitch, pace and timbre.
- **The radio, later:** its own volume slider comes with the comms log (M7); other crew members' calls sounding like they come from the cockpit beside the pilot (a second, roomier filter, as if the pilot's mic picks them up), or from wherever they are when the pilot isn't in the cockpit.
- **Fenna's voice, again with a better model:** Fenna (the cook) is a Black woman; voice design didn't make her sound like one (2026-10-26: "a Black woman" in the description changed nothing; named accents, Southern, Caribbean, Nigerian, and a timbre-only description came closer), so her English anchor is the closest take, a soft Southern accent, and she has no German voice yet (with German voices, she speaks her English lines, the fallback for a line without a recording). Replace both once a model with a license that fits can design such a voice in English and German.
- **Strained or urgent anchors** for characters who need them (a second anchor per language, picked per line).
- **AI-driven NPCs** stay a later, separate experiment (they share the GPU with rendering and need guardrails).
