# The crew (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 13 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. The eight crew members stand at their posts with their first topics and their own models, call in over the radio when something happens (see [NPCs](../GAME_DESIGN.md#5-spaceship-hub)); what's left:

- **Crew joining** (with the quests, see [`quests.md`](quests.md)): stations keep working without crew, so nothing gates progress behind finding someone; joining adds the person: their topics, explanations, quest chains and reputation. Aboard from the start are Brannik, Tamsin, Okoro and Ilse (the ship can't fly, fight or heal without them); Dace, Wren and Hollis join early (Torvane and Waypost Ostra, first quests), Fenna later at Ostra. Until the quests exist, everyone is aboard at their post. Story topics and quests come with the quest framework.
- **More transmissions:** each new event is lines in the profiles of those it concerns plus one hook (e.g. the first nest destroyed, the first alloy, the apex killed, a crew member joining); during a run only quest steps and story moments (with the quests), mostly from the pilot.
- **Co-op:** the crew is the host's, like the ship: anyone can talk to them; what's been heard is the host's story flags, the transmissions' turns are saved with the ship.

## Crew life aboard

Decided 2026-10-26, built in four PRs (A, the lines, and B, walking aboard, are built, see [NPCs](../GAME_DESIGN.md#5-spaceship-hub); C and D below, each after the one before). All values **TBD (playtest)**.

- **C. Little things to do:** clips every crew model shares (made once in the pose code): looking around over the shoulder, stretching the back, checking something on the wrist, tapping at a console in front; played now and then like Nell's work (3–8 s apart, never twice in a row), never while a player is close. Each crew member also has two or three work spots in their room (e.g. Tamsin at the holo table's near edge and at the bridge window, Brannik at the weapon wall and at the lockers) and walks between them every 20–40 s, tapping or looking around there; their home spot (where players find them first) is one of them, and every spot stays off the way in. Work with props of each person's own (Dace grinding at the bench, Fenna at the stove) stays with their own round ([`people.md`](people.md)).
- **D. Brannik's tour:** first the lift for the crew: a link between the decks on the ship's navigation that they ride like a player (calling the platform, stepping on, riding, stepping off; moved here from B, since the tour is the first to need it). On a new game Brannik waits in the corridor in front of the player cabins instead of the armory; his greeting there offers the tour ("Show me the ship", "Later"), without treating the player as new aboard (the crew knows them). "Later" sends him back to the armory, where "Show me the ship" stays among his topics, so the tour can be asked for any time. The route: the mess hall (Fenna), the bridge (Tamsin, the navigator), the cockpit (the flight console), the lift down, the medical bay (Ilse), the engine room (Okoro), the four rooms along the lower corridor (Hollis' miners, the skills, Wren's armor, Dace's weapons), the shooting range, and his armory, where the tour ends with his greeting; the cargo bay and the ramp he points at from there. About 12 stops, a line or two each. He walks ahead to the next stop and waits until the player is within 4 m, says that stop's line (bubble and voice) and walks on. Talking to him during the tour shows "Go on" and "End the tour" (ending sends him to the armory, the topic stays); if the player walks off (farther than 25 m for 30 s) he ends it by himself. In co-op, whoever asked leads it, others may tag along.
