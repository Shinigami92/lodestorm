# Waypost Ostra (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 12 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. Built (step 12): docking, leaving, the saved position, the greybox map with the camera cut-away and floor guides, its furniture, the named NPCs with their first topics, the ambient colonists and the ore exchange, see Stations and outposts in [Spaceship](../design/ship.md).

- **The colony's hub:** Waypost Ostra is an orbital station where the colony's people, its council and its supply depot are; the colony's settlements on the surface are outposts (later). The players supply it, and when the colony has to leave at the end, it leaves from here. A second station comes later with the story (the lost expedition's abandoned base, deeper in, e.g. in orbit around Gaulm); inhabited outposts (handcrafted landing sites without enemies) later still.
- **Why players go there:** the story and quest givers outside the crew (the council, other prospecting crews, colonists), crew members who join (some are met there first, see [`crew.md`](crew.md)), the ore exchange, deliveries to the colony (see [`quests.md`](quests.md)), and walking around together in co-op between runs. It never gates progress (the heat shield does) and is reachable from the start, without a tier.
- **The map's rooms get their things** with the steps that need them: the quest notice board in the concourse (step 16); the greybox furniture and the exchange's hoppers are built, their models come with the art pass.
- **No shops:** ores are the only currency and the ship has the upgrade stations, so stalls and vendors are scenery and story. Instead, station NPCs **ask for things**: requests to bring an amount of an ore or a refined alloy, handed in at them like any quest delivery (see [`quests.md`](quests.md)).
- **Who's there:** the named NPCs and the ambient colonists are built (see [`design/station.md`](../design/station.md)). Still to come: Kade's deliveries, Selin's story and the others' quests (step 16); their stories in [`story.md`](story.md).
- **People sit, not only walk** (decided 2026-10-25, built, see Ambient colonists in [`design/station.md`](../design/station.md)): colonists sit for good on a bench or in a booth, or sit down for a while at a seat on their walk, seated ones still remarking without turning or getting up. Still to come: more seats as the rooms get their things (the council's chairs, the depot), the crew sitting at their posts, and sitting down and getting up as clips of their own instead of a blend.
- **Smarter NPCs later** (an idea, not decided): colonists might follow a day routine (work, the bar, the gallery, sleep, by the station's clock) instead of fixed routes, and some might become talkable (a `Colonist` with a dialogue). The profiles already hold a role and remarks, so a routine and topics can be added to them.
- **Co-op:** the station is part of the host's session like the ship: guests can join while docked and appear aboard or in the hangar. No public lobby with strangers.
- **Look:** the same industrial, weathered human tech as the ship, but lived-in and crowded: cargo containers, cables, patched hull plates, warm work lights against the dark, colonists in worn suits; big windows show the planet below and space. Concept images first (ComfyUI), then greybox.
