# Destroyable nests (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17, built in M4 step 12 (see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). All values **TBD (playtest)**. Today a nest can't be hurt: it hatches its guards, then hunters and raiders, escalating until the storm (see [Combat & aliens](../GAME_DESIGN.md#10-combat--aliens)).

- **Destroying one:** a nest has health, and every weapon hurts it, ranged and melee, so a melee-only loadout can clear nests too. Greybox 600 HP (about 8 s of blaster fire at 8 shots a second, longer while its defenders come). It has armor like the aliens (a flat reduction per hit, at least 25 % gets through, see [`aliens.md`](aliens.md)), so heavy hits and explosives (the seismic launcher, the charge layer, see [`weapons.md`](weapons.md)) are better, never required.
- **It fights back:** at two thirds and one third of its health it bursts: it spews 3 aliens at once (its tier's strains), after a 1 s warning (the mouth swells and glows white). Its guards, if still alive, come to defend it. So clearing a nest is a short fight, not a free kill.
- **Its state, without a health bar:** three stages readable from the camera: cracks open in its plates after each burst and the mouth's glow flickers lower. When it dies, it collapses inwards, the mouth goes dark, and the slime around it dries and greys over a few seconds; a dark husk stays as a landmark for the rest of the run. The damage readout skill shows numbers on it like on aliens.
- **The hive answers:** its hunters and raiders stop coming for the rest of the run (the aliens it already hatched stay), but every other nest hatches its next hunter right away, a short surge. So it pays off over the run but costs something right then.
- **Clearing a whole location** is allowed: with every nest gone, no more hunters or raiders hatch, only what's still out there. With about 10 nests that's a big effort, and the storm still ends the run, so it's a way to play, not an exploit.
- **No drops:** nothing to pick up (aliens and nests drop nothing, ore only comes from mining). It pays off in safety (the ore next to nests becomes safe to mine), fewer hunters and raiders, and quest progress (the "clear nests" goal, see [`quests.md`](quests.md)).
- **Next visit:** every visit generates a new section of the region, so the nests are back; quest steps can add their own nest groups.
- **Scaling:** nest health scales like alien health, +30 % per tier above 1 (see Tiers in [Missions](../GAME_DESIGN.md#6-missions)), and with the player count in co-op. Bigger hive nests on later tiers come with the hatch tables (see [`aliens.md`](aliens.md)).
- **Finding them** stays exploring: as today, the location hologram doesn't show nests and nests get no silhouette behind cliffs, alive or destroyed; a quest's nests get amber beams.
- **For the code:** a signal when a nest is destroyed, which the quests' "clear nests" goal counts later; a debug overlay shortcut that damages every nest; tests for the health and armor, the bursts, the stages, the hunters and raiders stopping and the surge.
