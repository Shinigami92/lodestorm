# The story (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17 as the campaign's outline; each act is written in its own writing round before its quests are built (M4 step 16 D and on, see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). The setting and how it's told: see [Story and setting](../design/story.md); the world around it (the lane, the charter, the hive's origin, the colony's numbers): see [`world.md`](world.md); how quests carry it: see [`quests.md`](quests.md).

## Tone

Grounded: working people in a hard place, dry humor among the crew, no chosen one, no villain monologues. The players are prospectors doing a job that slowly becomes the colony's lifeline. The dread grows inward with the star: early acts are about supplies and a missing expedition, later ones about getting everyone out.

## What the colony builds

**A new lane beacon, to leave.** Lanes lie at the systems' edges, and a crossing needs a beacon at each end to lock onto; without one, ships jump blind and don't come out. The lane watch's beacon has a core of Aethrine, the only crystal that holds a lane's tune, and Kesh's flares are burning it out: it weakens in act 2, goes dark in act 3 (no lane ship comes any more) and the colony can't leave. A new core needs fresh Aethrine from Zairen, nearest the star. So the colony builds a new beacon at the lane watch, its core of Zairen's Aethrine inside a flare shield made from the six signature ores and their alloys, so that what killed the old one can't kill this one. The players' heat shield is the flare shield's small prototype: every plate they install (see Opening planets in [Missions](../design/missions.md)) teaches Okoro what the big one needs, which ties the heat shield's gate to the story's goal. The convoy is the colony's haulers (they crossed the lane on their way in); once the beacon is lit, they cross a few at a time over days while it holds against the rising flare, and the players' ship crosses last (the ending's camera moment). The big flare is the deadline: after it, the system is scorched and nothing is left to leave from. Calder had found the beacon failing (in an old survey reading) and went to Zairen for Aethrine, which is why the expedition went inward and was caught there (see [`world.md`](world.md)).

## The hive and the star

The hive feeds on the rare ores' radiation, which is why it grows stronger inwards and why nests sit by rich ore; the star's flares (the storms) drive it into a frenzy (the end-of-run frenzy, see [`aliens.md`](aliens.md)). Nobody knows at the start; the expedition found out.

## The expedition

A science expedition led by **Calder**, sent ahead of the colony. They understood the flare cycle, built the prototype weapons from signature ores to survive the hive (see [`weapons.md`](weapons.md)), and went inward to test a shield design near Zairen, where an early flare caught them. Their base orbiting Gaulm is abandoned, with one survivor, **Pell**, half-frozen in a failing cryo bay, found in act 4: the one person who saw the inner planets. Pell then speaks over the radio and gives quests at the base (no cabin aboard).

## The six acts

| Act | Body | What happens | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Torvane (and Lisk) | **The job:** arrival at Waypost Ostra, the council hires the crew for supply runs; Brannik's first jobs; the first expedition camp in Hollow Mire and Calder's first log; the crew gathered (Dace, Wren, Hollis and Fenna join) | heat shield MK2 (Vehra), with the whole crew aboard |
| 2 | Vehra | **The trail:** the expedition's frozen dropship on Hoarfrost Ridge; Okoro notices the storms follow a cycle; the storms get worse | MK3 (Cassor) |
| 3 | Cassor (and Mirr) | **The warning:** an expedition outpost in the salt with star readings; the hive spreading outward from the inner planets; the beacon goes dark and the lane ship doesn't come; the council doesn't want panic | MK4 (Gaulm's moons) |
| 4 | Orrel and Iska | **The base:** docking at the expedition's base, finding Pell; the flare forecast becomes a date, not a feeling; the colony decides to leave, and a new beacon behind a flare shield becomes the plan | MK5 (Skelde) |
| 5 | Skelde | **The build:** gathering Noctile and the alloys for the beacon's flare shield at Ostra, carried to the lane watch; the rival crew joins the effort; Pell's route to Calder's last site | MK6 (Zairen) |
| 6 | Zairen | **The way out:** Calder's last site and the final prototype (Zenith); the last Aethrine under worsening flares, the new beacon's core; lighting it at the lane watch, the convoy's crossing, the players' ship last to cross | the ending (the crossing as its camera moment) |

No real-time deadline: the flare is a date in the story, and the last act begins when the players are ready.

Acts 1–4 each bring a miner's cradle (in the middle of the act) and a furnace as main-quest rewards, paid in parts for the stripped ship (see [`balance.md`](balance.md)); each act's writing round says who hands them over.

## People

- **Personal threads,** told through each crew member's side quests (see [`crew.md`](crew.md); each person's whole sheet: see [`characters.md`](characters.md)):
  - **Brannik:** his sister was on the expedition; his chain follows her trail, and act 4 tells him what happened (her name is Maren).
  - **Tamsin:** flew the colony's haulers in; wants everyone out, nervous of the storms.
  - **Okoro:** reads the star; the shield is his obsession; proud, wrong once (act 3).
  - **Ilse:** counts the cost; distrusts the council's calm.
  - **Dace:** rebuilds the expedition's prototypes and wonders what they were made against.
  - **Wren:** born at Ostra, never off the station before the crew.
  - **Hollis:** the oldest prospector in the system; knew Calder; gruff, kind.
  - **Fenna:** hears everything; the rumor that turns out true (act 3).
- **Waypost Ostra** (see [`station.md`](station.md)): council member **Selin** (carries the main story with the pilot), depot master **Kade**, rival prospector **Rook** (captain of the rival crew), bartender **Nell**.
- **Personal arcs move together:** each crew member has a beat in every act, not one chain finished early and silent afterwards, but unevenly: 2–3 big beats each (a quest chain's step) and small ones between (a new topic, a talk). The campaign's big three: Brannik's sister, Okoro's mistake (act 3) and Hollis having known Calder.
- **The crew among themselves:** they have opinions about each other, grounded and small, resolved over the acts, none changing gameplay: Ilse and Selin distrust each other, Okoro and Dace argue about the prototypes, Wren looks up to Tamsin, Hollis teases Brannik, Fenna knows everyone's business.
- **Nobody dies:** no one of the crew or at Ostra dies; the losses are the expedition's (the past) and colonists out of sight. Nobody of the crew leaves either; the one who stays behind is Selin, in Ostra's shelter with those who won't leave (act 6).
- **The players** are silent, nameless prospectors with no backstory beyond being the crew's prospectors; the crew says "you" (du/ihr). No choices in conversations for now (every choice doubles the lines and voices, and in co-op only one player picks); a second look once act 1 is written.
- **Pell** (from act 4) recovers at Ostra after the rescue (Ilse brings them back; talkable there), goes along to the base and the lane watch as a passenger for quest steps, speaks over the radio during runs and leaves messages on the quest board: the voice from further in for acts 5–6.

## Between missions

Decided 2026-10-27. All values **TBD (playtest)**. About 120 runs make about 20 per planet, most of them mining for the next heat shield plate; the main steps and side quests alone leave many returns where nothing has changed. So the world reacts between runs:

- **Flying anywhere, any time:** players fly to any open body or station whenever they want, so acts aren't places: a beat waits for being on the act's planet only when it's about a place (a site, a first landing), otherwise for flags, progress and returns, wherever the return is. A **return** is coming back aboard after a run or docking at a station. Each act has one or two steps or side quests back on earlier bodies (an outpost to evacuate, Rook's distress call on Vehra, a log Okoro wants re-read on site). When a quest step or beat waits at a station, the navigator shows an amber diamond on the station, the person waiting has theirs, and they call once (a message on the quest board's Messages tab; built in 16 A3b, see Between missions in [Story](../design/story.md)). Ore hand-ins stay in person (a reason to dock); news that needs no meeting comes as a message. Dockings get beats of their own (a new remark, a talk at the bar, Kade's next request), so frequent dockings don't repeat; the docking flight stays as it is.
- **Something new on every return:** something small each time the players get back aboard (a new topic, a talk, a message, a crew member waiting somewhere with an amber marker), something bigger (a quest step, a gathering) about every 3–4 runs.
- **The story director** (built in 16 A3a, see Between missions in [Story](../design/story.md)) picks it: **beats** live in data (who, where, what it waits for, its priority, once or repeatable), and on each return the director puts out the best one it can (at most one or two waiting at a time, the rest later). A beat waits for conditions, each a Resource of its own like the quests' goals: story flags and quest steps, the act, runs since the last beat, the heat shield plate paid (a third, two thirds), an ore found for the first time, a region's first visit, a crew member's reputation, a fall. Never the wall clock. Quests stay their own system; "something on every return" is one setting. A new planet, NPC or star system is more data.
- **The heat shield plate as story:** beats at about a third and two thirds paid (Okoro testing a sample, the plate's first glow, the pilot asking what's further in), carried by Okoro with one other crew member per act; installing it ends the act.
- **Overheard talks,** the main new kind of content (built in 16 B5, see Between missions in [Story](../design/story.md)): still to come are the talks themselves, 2–4 per act for each pair that matters, mostly crew among themselves, a few at Ostra (their talk spots with them), in co-op heard by everyone close; the important ones kept for the quest board's Logs tab (16 D, decided 2026-10-30), the others only overheard.
- **Messages on the quest board** (built in 16 A3b, see Between missions in [Story](../design/story.md)): still to come are the messages themselves, from Ostra (Selin, Kade, colonists, later Pell), with each act's content. The radio itself stays quiet; the comms log's replays of transmissions join it later (M7).
- **Crew gatherings:** at each act's end, after the heat shield plate is installed and before the first flight to the new planet, the crew meets in the mess hall and sits around the table; a player sitting down or standing by starts it, and each says a line or two, voiced. The game's cutscene without a cutscene; it needs the crew sitting and the table's seats.
- **Things to do aboard:** Brannik's range challenges (a target course per weapon, a time to beat, lines about it, no reward beyond them) and Fenna's dish per planet (a sample found in a region, a meal at the table like a small gathering); Hollis' tinkering later.
- **Incidents aboard:** one or two per act, put out by the director as side quests (a power fault, a crew member sick for a day so the med beds run without them); never blocking a station, nothing alive loose aboard (no fighting aboard).
- **Out there and at Ostra:** the station changes with every act, the colonists get new remarks and a few threads of their own, Rook's crew leaves traces in the regions, and the colony's surface outposts come one per planet (see [`station.md`](station.md)).
- **Where it's built:** the director (16 A3a) and the messages (16 A3b) with the quest framework, overheard talks in 16 B5, act 1's content using all of them in its writing round (16 D); gatherings and the activities once the crew can sit.

## After the ending

The game goes on in the same save (no reset): the flare has passed, the star calms for a while, and the crew stays as the system's last prospectors. Every region gets an "after the flare" danger above hard (a tier above 6, the MK cap one higher), rarer ore yields, the hive at its strongest, and requests keep coming from Ostra's skeleton crew. Details in a later round.

## Writing

Each act gets its own writing round: the quests' steps, every line in English and German by the dialogue rules, the radio lines, and the logs of that act's regions; the first one before M4 step 16 D. It also fills the act's time between missions (see Between missions): about 6 main steps, about 8 personal beats, about 6 overheard talks, about 6 messages, the gathering, Nell's rumors, Ostra's change and the colonists' remarks, about 150–200 lines per act in English and German (a target to check against the voices' render time; act 1 shows whether it holds).
