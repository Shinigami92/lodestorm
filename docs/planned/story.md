# The story (planned)

Decided 2026-10-17 as the campaign's outline; each act is written in its own writing round before its quests are built (M4 step 15 D and on, see [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)). The setting and how it's told: see [Story and setting](../GAME_DESIGN.md#story-and-setting-draft); how quests carry it: see [`quests.md`](quests.md).

## Tone

Grounded: working people in a hard place, dry humor among the crew, no chosen one, no villain monologues. The players are prospectors doing a job that slowly becomes the colony's lifeline. The dread grows inward with the star: early acts are about supplies and a missing expedition, later ones about getting everyone out.

## What the colony builds

**A flare shield, to leave.** The colony came on slow haulers and can't outrun the big flare; the only fast way out is a slingshot around Kesh itself, close enough to burn any hull. So the colony builds a flare shield for its convoy, from the six signature ores and their alloys. The players' heat shield is its small prototype: every plate they install (see [`opening-planets.md`](opening-planets.md)) teaches Okoro what the big one needs, which ties the campaign's only gate to the story's goal.

## The hive and the star

The hive feeds on the rare ores' radiation, which is why it grows stronger inwards and why nests sit by rich ore; the star's flares (the storms) drive it into a frenzy (the end-of-run frenzy, see [`aliens.md`](aliens.md)). Nobody knows at the start; the expedition found out.

## The expedition

A science expedition led by **Calder**, sent ahead of the colony. They understood the flare cycle, built the prototype weapons from signature ores to survive the hive (see [`weapons.md`](weapons.md)), and went inward to test a shield design near Zairen, where an early flare caught them. Their base orbiting Gaulm is abandoned, with one survivor, **Pell**, half-frozen in a failing cryo bay, found in act 4: the one person who saw the inner planets. Pell then speaks over the radio and gives quests at the base (no cabin aboard).

## The six acts

| Act | Body | What happens | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Torvane (and Lisk) | **The job:** arrival at Waypost Ostra, the council hires the crew for supply runs; Brannik's first jobs; the first expedition camp in Hollow Mire and Calder's first log | heat shield MK2 (Vehra) |
| 2 | Vehra | **The trail:** the expedition's frozen dropship on Hoarfrost Ridge; Okoro notices the storms follow a cycle; the storms get worse | MK3 (Cassor) |
| 3 | Cassor (and Mirr) | **The warning:** an expedition outpost in the salt with star readings; the hive spreading inward; the council doesn't want panic | MK4 (Gaulm's moons) |
| 4 | Orrel and Iska | **The base:** docking at the expedition's base, finding Pell; the flare forecast becomes a date, not a feeling; the colony decides to leave, and the flare shield becomes the plan | MK5 (Skelde) |
| 5 | Skelde | **The build:** gathering Noctile and the alloys for the shield at Ostra; the rival crew joins the effort; Pell's route to Calder's last site | MK6 (Zairen) |
| 6 | Zairen | **The way out:** Calder's last site and the final prototype (Zenith); the last Aethrine under worsening flares; the convoy's departure, the players' ship last to leave | the ending (the take-off as its camera moment) |

No real-time deadline: the flare is a date in the story, and the last act begins when the players are ready.

## People

- **Personal threads,** told through each crew member's side quests (see [`crew.md`](crew.md)):
  - **Brannik:** his sister was on the expedition; his chain follows her trail, and act 4 tells him what happened (her name comes with his writing round).
  - **Tamsin:** flew the colony's haulers in; wants everyone out, nervous of the storms.
  - **Okoro:** reads the star; the shield is his obsession; proud, wrong once (act 3).
  - **Ilse:** counts the cost; distrusts the council's calm.
  - **Dace:** rebuilds the expedition's prototypes and wonders what they were made against.
  - **Wren:** born at Ostra, never off the station before the crew.
  - **Hollis:** the oldest prospector in the system; knew Calder; gruff, kind.
  - **Fenna:** hears everything; the rumor that turns out true (act 3).
- **Waypost Ostra** (see [`station.md`](station.md)): council member **Selin** (carries the main story with the pilot), depot master **Kade**, rival prospector **Rook** (captain of the rival crew), bartender **Nell**.

## After the ending

The game goes on in the same save (no reset): the flare has passed, the star calms for a while, and the crew stays as the system's last prospectors. Every region gets an "after the flare" danger above hard (a tier above 6, the MK cap one higher), rarer ore yields, the hive at its strongest, and requests keep coming from Ostra's skeleton crew. Details in a later round.

## Writing

Each act gets its own writing round: the quests' steps, every line in English and German by the dialogue rules, the radio lines, and the logs of that act's regions; the first one before M4 step 15 D.
